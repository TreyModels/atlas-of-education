## 4. What the observed-path failure means for the next model line

### 4.1 What failed, and what did not

**What failed is one margin, and it is named.** The repaired class reproduces **neither** the level **nor** the path,
but the reconciliation localises the loss to a single behavioural object: the **aggregate participation decision**
inside the tier block. Its consequence is a **demand share of 0.900000** where the frozen four-state block gives
**0.995644**, and an **unfilled** margin that removes a further 10.00 pp of the cohort (V17, V18). **Whether that
consequence is a defect or a specified feature is precisely what EXP-16 must classify** — a block that re-decides
school-versus-outside where the frozen block already decides it is a **duplicated parameterisation**; a block that
applies A5's declared outside option to the tier menu is a **specified feature whose level consequence is real**. The
Council does not assume the answer; it requires it to be measured. At primary 2024 that is the whole gap: `1.10 × 0.800000 × 0.9233395869 =
81.25388365` against an observed **98.30461884** — **17.0507 pp**, with **survival identical in both arms**
(0.9233395869) and the convention identical (`m = 1.10`). **This is a statement about the model's own accounting —
not about the Philippines, and not a causal claim about participation anywhere.**

**What did *not* fail — and this matters for the next line.** (i) **Representation**: unfilled Tier O places and a
contested Tier H gate coexist in **15/15** anchor years, in every arm, including the ones that miss the path by 40 pp.
(ii) **The survival repair**: removing v1.5's tier survival multiplier recovered primary **51.63 → 81.25**, lower 2024
**51.57 → 74.67**, upper **44.89 → 57.41**. The survival exponent was the v1.5 collapse, and it is gone; there is no
survival side left to "repair". (iii) **The guards**: projection order, counterfactual identity, schooling identity,
lower register and no-fit audit all pass — the *machinery* is now trustworthy, which is why the residual failure is
interpretable at all. (iv) **The lower series** remains short through **two** channels (D140), and its survival
channel **cannot** close at the realised coverage.

**And one hypothesis is spent.** "Repair survival / repair the lower series" was the cycle-15 working hypothesis. Its
survival half is **already executed** (the multiplier removal) and its lower half is **joint** and, at the realised
coverage, **not reachable by the survival lever alone**. A successor that "repairs survival" would have nothing to
do.

### 4.2 The four options, ruled

- **(a) A declared survival/lower-series repair with the observed path as the only target — ADOPTED, NARROWED and
  CONDITIONAL.** Narrowed because the survival half is spent and the lower half is not a survival repair; what remains
  is the **participation margin** (and, for the lower series, a **coverage** object — EXP-17). Conditional because the
  admissibility of "repair" here turns on **provenance**: a repair that *unifies a duplicated parameterisation* is a
  declaration; a repair that *moves a declared behavioural value until the path fits* is a fit, and the Council will
  not authorise it. EXP-16 measures which one this is **before** any repair is predeclared.
- **(b) Retreat to the pipeline-object/κ question with the model descriptive only — REJECTED as the *next* step,
  ADOPTED as the pre-committed fallback.** Rejected now because the failure is *identified to one object*: a retreat
  taken before measuring whether that object is a defect would abandon a question one cheap cell can settle, and the
  representational gain (a class that holds the coexistence) would be published without its level verdict. Adopted as
  the fallback **by rule**: if EXP-16 returns `STRUCTURAL-MENU`, or EXP-18 is VOID, or EXP-18 misses the bar, the
  programme's model line becomes **descriptive** — the class is published as a representation result, κ as a
  within-window description, v1.2 as a placement instrument — and no further generator re-specification is admitted
  this phase.
- **(c) A measurement-first line (D119/D135/D140) — PARTIALLY ADOPTED, not as the line.** The places object is
  **DATA BLOCKED** on disk (`STATE_CAPACITY.csv`: *number of public schools by year*, *classroom construction
  completed*, *number of classrooms* all `unable`), so a measurement-first line would stall this cycle. Measurement
  enters as **one bounded proxy cell** (EXP-19) and as the **HELD ledger** in the register (EXP-20), not as the line.
- **(d) Anything else — NOT ADMITTED.** No new layer (D116), no re-tune of A3-D (D130), no re-opening of C1–C4, no
  paper-test slate (D113b), no HKG run (D133).

### 4.3 The decision tree (pre-committed, so the outcome cannot be negotiated afterwards)

```
EXP-16 PARTPROV (count-blind)
├── DUPLICATED-PRIMITIVES  → EXP-18 PART-REPAIR licensed (one declaration-consistency change, observed path only)
│     ├── bar met (≥17/33, ±5 y)      → class becomes a CANDIDATE LEVEL MODEL; next question = what sets the
│     │                                  participation primitives and the coverage path (measurement, EXP-19)
│     └── bar missed                  → generator line CLOSED at representation-only; line becomes (b)+(c)
├── MIXED                  → only the declaration-consistency part may move; the menu-consequence part is declared
│                            and reported as a sensitivity; EXP-18 licensed under the same closing rule
└── STRUCTURAL-MENU        → EXP-18 VOID by its own rule; generator line CLOSED; line becomes (b)+(c)
```

**Why this is not a rescue.** The decision to *test provenance before repairing* is made **now**, and it is made
**because the failure is identified**, not because a repair is expected to succeed. The one thing a rescue cannot
survive is a **closing rule fixed in advance** — and that is what the tree carries: a miss closes the line, with no
"one more specification".

### 4.4 Written critical-path justification (drift rule, §step-back 4)

The programme's first-order question is a **level** question (does the model reproduce *which* milestones cross and
*when*). After this batch the programme holds: a representation capability, a descriptive coefficient, a labelled
placement instrument, and a **localised level failure**. Two of those three (representation, placement) are already
at their defensible limit; the third is the only one that can move the first-order answer, and it is now a
**single measured question** rather than a search. Spending one cheap cell on it — with a pre-committed close — is
the highest-information move available on disk, and it is cheaper than the alternative (publish a level verdict whose
cause is known but unclassified).

## 6. Authoring rules added this cycle (all binding on the next predeclaration)

1. **Precedence rule (from EXP-13 C-1).** Every threshold block states the **order** in which its verdict labels are
   tested. Where two labels can both apply, the block says which one wins. A block that does not is read in the order
   printed, and the Council reads the **narrower** label.
2. **Resolution pretest (from EXP-14 C-2).** Any shock/scenario leg must declare, **before the run**, that the shock
   moves **≥ 1** declared cell/object; if it cannot, the leg is declared `UNFIRABLE-BY-DESIGN` and is **not scored**
   (never scored as a FAIL). A zero that is a resolution artefact may be **reported** but may not carry a verdict.
3. **Arm tuple + capability lint (from EXP-14 C-3, upgrading D134(4)).** Every leg names its arm by the **full switch
   tuple**, and the predeclaration must pass a **capability lint**: if a named object cannot exist in the named arm,
   the build **fails** rather than the leg scoring `NaN`.
4. **Count-blind rule (from EXP-14 C-5).** Where a diagnostic cell informs a later scored cell, the diagnostic may
   **not** compute the scored cell's verdict object. For the participation line: EXP-16 is count-blind by guard.
5. **Falsifier-arm audit (from EXP-11 C-1).** Every falsifier states, per arm, whether that arm **can** fire, and the
   expected direction of the statistic that fires it. A signed test on an unsigned object is a defect at freeze.
6. **Scope inheritance (from EXP-12 C-3 / EXP-14 C-4).** A disposition measured on one generator line does **not**
   carry to another without an explicit scope sentence. D139 is the worked example: it is correct for v1.2/v1.3 and
   **false** for the bimodal class at the anchor.
7. **No unit-mixing (from V23).** Cell counts, year counts and arm counts are never used interchangeably in one
   sentence; the denominator is written.
