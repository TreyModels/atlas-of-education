# REVIEW — PAGE composite (drawing on Page 2018; Miller & Page 2007) — brief_005

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: a "two-way" claim is a claim about which feedback channels generate the
aggregate path; the test is counterfactual removal, not correlation; provider entry changes the model class from
fixed supply to a supply response and must be validated against price/count dynamics. **COUNCIL
EXTRAPOLATION:** the assessment below is the composite's.

**M. MODEL RENDERING.** M1 channels: education→economy via human-capital elasticity with lag; economy→education via
returns/wages; shadow sector via demand + provider entry + price tatonnement + capacity dilution. M2 counterfactuals:
freeze schooling (is the job path still generated?), freeze economy (is attainment still generated?). M3 validation
targets for the new block: price levels, provider counts, teacher-tutor share. M4 the direction classifier is a
*within-model* decomposition. M5 nesting: this module is not massabm/floorworld; it is a new declared model. M6
falsifier: a channel that, when removed, changes nothing is a schedule. M7 KIDS: describe each country's actual
provider mix and gate structure before assigning capacity/fixed-cost values.

**A. VERDICTS.** **Two-way feedback: NOT IDENTIFIED — one side is a schedule.** The freeze-both test was run
properly and delivers the central negative: with schooling frozen, complex vacancies still rise strongly (PH
71.0→130.9; HK 172.8→235.8; KHM 32.3→80.0), and endogenous schooling adds only +4.5/+6.5/+3.1 jobs (≈3–4% of the
level). The job path is therefore overwhelmingly the declared demand trend. On the other side, freezing the economy
still lets attainment rise +0.028/+0.022/+0.024 (vs +0.067/+0.118/+0.077 live), a residual non-wage channel of
≈20–40% that the model attributes to the shadow sector. So the honest description is "education→economy is weak and
schedule-like; economy→education is genuine; a shadow-supply channel carries roughly a quarter to two-fifths of the
education rise." That is *not* a two-way model in the classical sense, and the report must not call it one.

**B. WHAT IS IMPOSED.** The provider-entry block: endogenous entry raises senior-secondary take-up by 54–67 pp
relative to fixed supply (PH 0.884 vs 0.219; HK 0.865 vs 0.327; KHM 0.887 vs 0.258), but in the same run prices fall
to their declared floor (0.30/0.55/0.13) and provider counts grow 5–90×. The ERROR_LOG admits the price/entry block
overshoots. That means the magnitude of the entry effect is an artifact of uncalibrated entry costs and capacities —
its *direction* is credible (supply response matters), its *size* is not. The teacher-tutor dominance (76/86/97%) is
generated by lower fixed cost/lower capacity, not fitted to provider data; treat as a structural possibility, not a
finding.

**C. ESTIMAND.** The direction decomposition is within-model (Ed_dir, Ec_dir), not an empirical estimate. The
classifier's power is one country: HK demand-led (+0.098, 2sd 0.014); PH and KHM are "neither identified" with
effects at ~0.08pp or below. One country out of three is a demonstration, not a pattern.

**D. TYPE.** "Demand-led" for HK is a model-class statement; it is not evidence that Hong Kong's expansion was
demand-led. Keep the hedge.

**E. STRONGEST DEFENSIBLE SENTENCE.** "In this module the education→economy channel is approximately a schedule
(3–4% of the job path), while the economy→education channel is genuine, with a shadow-supply residual of 20–40%; the
provider-entry block moves take-up in the right direction but its price dynamics are uncalibrated."

**F. NEGATIVE.** The strongest negative is not the collapse of the two-way claim; it is that the *one* channel that
does generate education (the shadow sector) has take-up levels set by a single fitted intercept, so the education
side's magnitude is also partly a construction.

**G2. STEEL-MAN.** The module is a genuine improvement in model class (feedback and provider supply replace
schedules), and the freeze-both test is the right instrument. A referee should credit the method even while rejecting
the results.

**H. ALTERNATIVE.** The residual attainment rise under frozen economy may be a first-order counterfactual artifact
(vacancies/beliefs held at t=0, not re-solved) — an upper bound, as the ERROR_LOG says.

**I. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND.** Price and provider-count calibration; a recovery test that shows the module can
distinguish demand-led from education-led when the truth is known; stage-matched shadow checks.
