# REVIEW — CARTWRIGHT composite (drawing on Cartwright 1999; Cartwright & Hardie 2012) — brief_005

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: capacities are local; a model's output is an implication of its coded structure;
evidence must match the claim it is used for; scope conditions and missing evidence must be stated, not inferred.
**COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the sign-off audit is the composite's.

**M. MODEL RENDERING.** For each claim: what capacity, under what conditions, with what observation would it show up
outside the model? M1 capacities coded here: a demand-trend schedule for jobs; returns→schooling; shadow supply
entry; a one-parameter household-demand intercept. M2 all hand-set. M3 the only fitted moment is HK 0.85. M4 model
implications only. M5 the module is new, not nested in massabm. M6 what would refute each claim. M7 KIDS: describe
the country's gate/provider structure before assigning capacity values.

**A. VERDICTS.** **Sign-off: NOT as validation; sign-off as an internally coherent stylized module with one
calibrated moment and one strong negative.** Claim-by-claim:
- **Two-way feedback: NOT ESTABLISHED (asymmetry is the finding, not a defect).** Job side ≈ schedule (+3–4%);
  education side genuine; residual shadow channel 20–40% (upper bound per ERROR_LOG). Report the asymmetry; do not
  claim a two-way model.
- **Shadow education where the literature says: NOT ESTABLISHED.** One of eleven literature shadow targets is fitted
  (HK 0.85); the rest are unused; stage-matched PH/KHM levels are contradicted. The one "match" is a restatement.
- **Direction: HK demand-led only.** PH/KHM "neither" with effects ≈0 — that is a null, not a pattern. One country
  is a demonstration.
- **Provider entry/teacher-tutor dominance: structural possibility, not a result.** Prices at floor and 5–90×
  provider growth make the magnitude non-credible; the direction (supply response matters) is credible.
- **Cohort flows: UNABLE TO ASSESS** (ledger pending); internal completion misses reported.
- **Positional channel: null in this module.** State it as a null, not as evidence against the paper's claim.

**B. IMPOSED VS GENERATED.** The HK take-up is generated *by the fit*: one intercept solved to one target. That is
exactly the loop's stop rule 5 ("the target was coded as a parameter"). It should be labelled a calibration and
excluded from any incidence claim. Everything else in the shadow sector is generated from declared costs, capacities
and price dynamics — which is the right structure, currently uncalibrated.

**C. WHAT MAY BE SAID.** "In this module, education→economy is weak/schedule-like while economy→education is
genuine; provider entry can move take-up substantially but its price dynamics are uncalibrated; take-up can be
calibrated to one HK moment, while the cross-country shadow pattern is not identified; the positional channel is null
in this specification; cohort consistency and identification (holdout, recovery) are pending."

**D. WHAT MUST NOT BE SAID.** "Shadow education appears where the literature says"; "two-way feedback is
established"; "HK is demand-led" without the hedge; "PH/KHM shadow matches"; any iteration-2 result as a country
estimate; "the restatement test passed".

**E. AMENDMENTS (minimum).** (1) Report all 11 shadow targets in a check table with stage-matched comparisons and a
disclosed miss; (2) label HK 0.85 as the fitted moment and exclude it from incidence claims; (3) relabel the two-way
section as "asymmetric feedback"; (4) mark cohort flows, calibration table, holdout and recovery UNABLE TO ASSESS /
pending; (5) fix the register count (11, not 6) and the entry-effect range (54–67 pp), and state the price/entry
overshoot in the main text, not only the error log; (6) run the holdout and recovery before any "identified" word.

**F. NEGATIVE.** The most important result in the batch is the negative one: the job path is a schedule, so the
programme's "education–economy system" is, in this module, mostly an economy→education system with a shadow-sector
add-on. That is publishable as a negative/structural result and is more valuable than a fitted shadow level.

**G2. STEEL-MAN.** A referee could accept the module and still ask what it establishes: the answer is the method
(freeze-both, provider entry, honest target register) plus the negative map. That is a legitimate methods
contribution.

**H. ALTERNATIVE.** The residual attainment rise could be a first-order counterfactual artifact (frozen vacancies and
beliefs, not re-solved steady states) — as the error log admits; label it an upper bound.

**I. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND.** Stage-matched checks published as a miss table; holdout + recovery executed;
price/entry calibrated; then the module could support a conditional claim.
