# REVIEW — CARTWRIGHT composite (drawing on Cartwright 1999; Cartwright & Hardie 2012) — brief_012

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: a claim may use only the capacity its evidence establishes; an identity tells
you where a change is located, not what the located quantity is; scope conditions belong in the sentence.
**COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the language assessment is the composite's.

**A. WHAT T1 ESTABLISHES.** (i) In Korea 2023, spend per student rises 23.4% (reported series) from elementary to
high school while participation falls 19.6 pp. (ii) Under the registered hours convention, weekly hours fall
0.8 h and the spend rise is arithmetically located in spend per participating student-hour (+79.3%). (iii) Therefore
the paper's one-margin "more participation drives spend" description is contradicted by its own best case. That is a
real, narrow result — and it is enough to force a **descriptive** rewrite of the mechanism sentence.

**B. WHAT T1 DOES NOT ESTABLISH.** (i) A price. The "price" term is a constructed residual containing provider,
subject, purpose, quality and selection changes; the KEDI tables on disk cannot separate them. (ii) That hours fall —
the source's hours denominator is ambiguous ("students" vs participating students); if hours are per all students,
per-participant hours rise and the predeclared conjunction ("while hours fall") fails. (iii) Anything causal or
cross-country: 2023 cross-section, Korea only.

**C. PERMISSIBLE PAPER LANGUAGE (exact).**
> "In Korea (2023), spending per student rises 23.4% between elementary and high school even as participation falls
> from 86.0% to 66.4%. Because per-student spending is the product of participation and spending per participant, the
> increase cannot be a participation effect; in an exact log decomposition it is located entirely in spending per
> participating student-hour (+79.3% under the survey's reported weekly hours). That quantity is a composite of price,
> provider and subject mix, materials and quality, and the available tables do not separate them. The one-margin
> 'arms-race participation' mechanism is therefore withdrawn; the Korean case is described as an intensive-margin
> pattern whose price-versus-composition interpretation requires provider- and subject-level data."
The paper may **not** write: "price escalation", "families paid 79% more per hour", "the arms race is a price race",
or "hours of study fell" (until the denominator is verified). Both hours conventions must be reported in a footnote.

**D. VERDICT.** T1 forces the rewrite of the mechanism **description** (participation cannot carry the rise), not the
theory's general claim; the theory's legitimation claim is untouched and untested (T2 design-only). Mark the
decomposition as descriptive and conditional in the same paragraph.
