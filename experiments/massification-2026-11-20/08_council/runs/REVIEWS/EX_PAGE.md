# REVIEW — PAGE composite (drawing on Page 2018; Miller & Page 2007) — brief_012

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: an identity split tells you which term carries a change; it does not tell you
what that term means. A residual is the union of everything the decomposition was not designed to separate.
**COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the arithmetic assessment is the composite's.

**A. ALGEBRA.** Spend/student L = participation p × spend/participant e; e = hours/month h_m × price/hour q. So
ln L = ln p + ln h_m + ln q. With p 0.86→0.664 (Δln = −0.2587), h_m 32.5→29.03 (Δln = −0.1128), q 14,215→25,488
(Δln = +0.5839): sum = +0.2124 = Δln L ✓. The decomposition is exact; residual 0 carries no information. The
finding is therefore **definitional**: the rise sits in the q term, and q is constructed as e/h_m — it is not an
independent measurement of price. Call it "spend per participating student-hour", not price.

**B. WHAT THE RESIDUAL CONTAINS.** q = (all private-education spend per participant) ÷ (survey participation hours).
It absorbs: provider-mix shifts (hagwon vs 1:1 vs group), subject mix (general vs arts/physical; math/English vs
others), purpose mix (makeup/advance/next-level prep), school type (academic vs vocational), materials/textbooks,
quality/class-size changes, and participant selection (falling participation changes who is in the numerator and the
hours denominator). Any of these can produce +79% with zero true price change.

**C. ALTERNATIVE DECOMPOSITIONS TO RUN BEFORE ANY USE.** A1 hours-denominator verification (Table 2 notes; both
conventions; month multipliers 52/12, 48/12, 9.5/12). A2 provider mix × within-provider spend/hour by level. A3 general
vs arts/physical vs materials split by level. A4 purpose mix by level. A5 academic vs vocational high-school split.
A6 selection: participation and spend by income band by level. A7 quality proxy: 1:1/group/hagwon shares, class size
if available. A8 subject mix if reported. A9 report both the reported and derived per-student series.

**D. VERDICT.** The arithmetic is sound; the inference is not. T1 forces the paper to stop saying the rise is
participation (and, if hours are per participant, hours); it does not license "price escalation". Predeclare A2/A6 as
the decisive checks: if within-provider, within-income-band spend/hour is flat, the residual is composition.
