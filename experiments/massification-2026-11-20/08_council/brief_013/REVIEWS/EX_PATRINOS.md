# REVIEW — PATRINOS composite (identification; steel-man) — brief_013

**Identification verdict.** The design is right in kind and needs one discipline: **the flagships must be
reduced-form first**. E1's two-shock design is the only configuration that separates push from pull against each
other, but the interaction will be underpowered at national scale and depends on linking K-12 cohort exposure to
visa/licensure timing — data assembly risk. Run **E2 + E3 as the pair** (supply event study; published demand
design), predeclare their joint interpretation, and treat E1 as the integration test with an explicit power
calculation. Fair rivals must get the same populations, shocks, information and outcomes: human capital, world
culture, labour queue/credentialism, sectoral demand, migration — no rival may be structurally barred (STOP_RULES
R11). Common traps to predeclare: common shocks, reverse causality, selection, staggered TWFE bias, denominator
changes, immigration/remittances, policy endogeneity.

**Dissent:** the Council's E1-first ranking is right in principle; I would not block on it. The gate's requirement
(equivalence is publishable; no winner without discrimination) is the correct standard.
