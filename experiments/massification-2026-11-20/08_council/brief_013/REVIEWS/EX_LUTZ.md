# REVIEW — LUTZ composite (demography / education statistics) — brief_013

**Measurement verdict.** The gate is the right instrument, but the series choice needs one correction: **completion
(cohort attainment) must be primary, GER secondary.** GER denominators (age cohort, duration, repeaters) are the
recurring trap in this programme's history; completion CR.1/2/3 is defined on the population above graduation age and
is available for PHL/CHM/KHM/KOR (sparse), though **HKG and JPN do not report UIS completion** — so they are checks,
never fit countries. The KHM holdout is right (it has observed completion to 2023), but its series has definitional
breaks around 2011–2014 and 2019–2021; verify before it is used as a holdout, or the "failure" may be measurement.
Tolerances (5 pp in-sample, 8 pp out, ±5 y midpoint) are defensible for completion; ±5 y is tight for a logistic
midpoint but appropriate as a claim bar. The fit/check/holdout roles must be hashed into the runner's predeclaration
before fitting. Cohort conservation zero-tolerance is non-negotiable.

**Dissent:** I would add mean-years/attainment **stock** as a check where survey data exist (HK EDB 2025; KHM CLFS
2019) and never as a target. The universalization thresholds are conventions; state them as declared.
