# REVIEW — BRAY composite (drawing on Bray & Lykins 2012; Bray's comparative work; Bray & Zhang) — brief_003

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: shadow education is gate-anchored, culturally embedded, supplier-driven, and
re-routes after suppression; equity effects are first-order; bans and school-support policies interact with an
informal market. **COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the F3/F4 assessment is the composite's.

**M. MODEL RENDERING.** For a Double Reduction test I need: M1 households, mainstream school, paid tutors,
teacher-tutors, informal market; M2 support enters both score and *demand* (homework time, feedback, need);
M3 a declared substitution relation between support and paid tutoring; M4 observed paid and unpaid tutoring time and
spend by gate; M5 the model must not pre-commit the sign; M6 falsifier: a support regime that lowers paid spend at
fixed stakes; M7 KIDS: describe the 2021 Chinese bundle (ban + homework reduction + school support) before coding.

**A. VERDICTS.** F3 **WITHDRAWAL OF C10 STANDS**, but the positive half of F3 must be softened. E18b's substitution
channel is a one-parameter linear damping (`effect × clip(1 − ts·s, 0, 1)`, ts=1 halves, ts=2 fully displaces at
s=0.5 sd); at ts=2 paid spend goes to 0 in the universal arm — a construction, not a finding. The least
question-begging arm is C1 (`ts=1`, halving): 2,377 vs ban-only 3,669, Δ −1,291.5 (2sd 92.3) — *that* is the
defensible "substitution is possible" result. C2 (ts=2, spend 0) and C4 (25% capacity, ts=2) are best-case
constructions and must be labelled as such. F4 **SIGN OFF with the "unidentified" wording**, not "substitution
appears". F5/F6/F7/F8 → other reviewers.

**B. WHAT IS IMPOSED.** The E18b result is imposed by the damping parameter: with ts free, the sign is free. That is
not a defect — it is the correct demonstration that the direction is unidentified — but the summary must not say
"substitution appears once support has a demand channel" as if the channel were established; it should say "the sign
flips when support is declared to damp tutoring's marginal value, and the size of that damping is unidentified by any
data in this package". The E18 result (+723; apex 0.411 → 0.675) similarly reflects the coded additive module. The
pair is a *possibility map*, and the map's boundary is the interesting object.

**C. ESTIMAND.** Paid spend excludes unpaid family help, homework time and teacher tutoring — the channels the Double
Reduction hypothesis is actually about. A model that measures only paid spend cannot identify the policy. Say so.

**D. TYPE.** Neither E18 nor E18b is a measured Double Reduction effect. The draft is right about this; keep the
sentence and put it in the abstract, not only §3.

**E. STRONGEST DEFENSIBLE SENTENCE (China).** "In this model, a universal additive school-support module raises paid
tutoring spend at high-stakes gates (+18%), while a module declared to damp tutoring's marginal value lowers it; the
sign hinges on an unestimated substitution elasticity, so the effect of China's 2021 Double Reduction on paid
tutoring is not identified here and is not a tested policy effect."

**F. NEGATIVE.** The model cannot distinguish substitution from re-routing into unpaid/informal channels: when
`ts=2` zeroes paid spend, the model assumes the help moved into support, not that demand fell. That is exactly the
ambiguity the field argues about (Korea 1980, China 2021). Report it as ambiguity, not as a result.

**G2. STEEL-MAN.** A comparative shadow-education reading: tutoring persists because of status competition and
supplier adaptation, not because support fails to substitute. The E18b flip is consistent with either story; no
evidence distinguishes them.

**H. ALTERNATIVE.** Teacher-tutor channel (Jayachandran): support that changes teacher time can reduce paid tutoring
without any substitution parameter; runnable as an arm.

**I. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND.** A measured substitution elasticity (school homework/feedback intensity × paid
tutoring spend, policy-exposure design) or a ts-grid sign-boundary analysis showing the plausible range; a real
Chinese tutoring series with the ban and support introduced separately.
