# REVIEW — THELEN composite (drawing on Thelen 2004; Varieties of Capitalism) — brief_011

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: institutional configurations are complementary bundles; differentiation is a
configuration of training, certification and employer recognition, not a track count; responsiveness mixes capacity
with political coalitions. **COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the institutional assessment is the composite's.

**A. MERITS.** The typology's genuine value is as a **measurement agenda**: it proposes two dimensions that could be
operationalised and compared — differentiation (vocational/technical share, certified non-university pathways, track
mobility, returns by pathway, employer credential requirements) and responsiveness (capacity-binding flag: tax/GDP
13.1%, GDP pc US$1,706, 1990 secondary GER 47.3%, urban 40.2% from the scout typology; plus spending response R01 =
0.111 world/Asia 0.189 and reform events). That is a real comparative contribution if done, and it is currently only
asserted.

**B. WEAKNESSES OF THE MERITS.** The cells are under-identified: Singapore and Germany are not one regime
(different state–employer configurations), India and Brazil are not one (different informality/provider structures);
Korea's placement in Regime II rests on the SKY anecdote, not on measured status hierarchies; HKG — the case that fits
the mechanism — is omitted. A 2×2 with four heterogeneous cells is a labelling scheme until it has measures and
within-cell variation.

**C. WHAT IS WORTH TESTING.** (1) **Construct the two indices** for the six country cards plus the scout's 30-country
panel; test whether observed tutoring take-up (where available) and provider form fall in the predicted cells —
descriptive, cheap, and it makes the typology falsifiable. (2) **Within-country institutional change:** Korea's
vocational/status policies vs tutoring outcomes; Netherlands/Germany apprenticeship/recognition vs tutoring — policy
documents and cohort flows. (3) **HKG vs KOR as the natural pair** for the gate-architecture claim (rising vs falling
stage profile), with the 2003 caveat.

**D. WHAT TO CUT.** "Equivalent social legitimacy" of vocational routes (empirical claim, not narrative); the
2×2's implicit assumption that responsiveness is one thing (it is capacity + coalitions). Keep the typology as an
agenda and the institutional-complementarity point; drop the claim that it is already a validated classification.
