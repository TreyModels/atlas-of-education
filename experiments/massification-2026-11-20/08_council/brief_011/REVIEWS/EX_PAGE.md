# REVIEW — PAGE composite (drawing on Page 2018; Miller & Page 2007) — brief_011

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: a proposition is worth testing if a feasible observation can contradict it; the
cheapest decisive test usually decomposes an aggregate rather than adding data. **COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the test
ranking is the composite's.

**A. THE TESTABLE CORE.** Four propositions in the paper are falsifiable in principle: (i) bans re-route spend at
unchanged gate stakes; (ii) regime transition requires status recalibration; (iii) Regime I is misclassified if
tutoring is required to clear gates; (iv) ritual persists where marginal learning returns ≈0 and motives are
normative. Of these, (iv) is the paper's distinctive claim and the hardest to test; (i) and (iii) are testable with
survey/policy data; (ii) needs institutional comparison.

**B. CHEAPEST DECISIVE TEST — KOREA DECOMPOSITION.** The paper's Regime II mechanism is an "arms race" claim. On-disk
official 2023 gives participation, hours and spend by level; spend = participation × hours × price/hour. Compute the
implied price/hour: elementary ≈ 398k/(7.5×4.33) ≈ 12.3k won/hour; high ≈ 491k/(6.7×4.33) ≈ 16.9k won/hour (~+37%).
So Korea's spend rise is a price story while participation falls and hours fall slightly. That falsifies a simple
"more hours of competition" mechanism and supports the two/three-margin rewrite. Cost: near-zero (data on disk);
falsifier: price/hour flat while spend rises (then the rise is hours or participants).

**C. THE NOVEL CLAIM'S TEST.** Legitimation: a vignette survey experiment (randomise described tutoring investment and
outcome; measure perceived fairness/meritocracy, N≈600–1,000 online) is the cheapest way to falsify (iv)-adjacent
claims. Predeclare: tutoring-investment arm raises fairness ratings by ≥0.2 SD vs no-investment; null kills the
legitimation mechanism as stated. Cost: low (one survey round, ~2–4 weeks).

**D. WHAT IS NOT WORTH TESTING.** The "extended phenotype" metaphor (no falsifier); the typology as a 2×2 classification
without measures (it will always fit post hoc); the Regime I narrative (no data).

**E. ONE FIRST.** Korea decomposition: free, decisive for the mechanism, and it tells the author whether to keep or
rewrite Regime II. If the goal is the theory's novelty rather than the mechanism description, run the vignette
experiment first; the two are complementary, not substitutes.
