**A. VERDICT — I concur that the limits are established, with one important scope condition.** The shadow-incidence
verdict rests on targets that are themselves weak: Korea official (high), Hong Kong 2003 (medium-high, 6-school quota),

available targets, in this model class". The difference matters for the next team, and for the paper: the measurement
programme is the asset that would let anyone test the phenomenon properly.
