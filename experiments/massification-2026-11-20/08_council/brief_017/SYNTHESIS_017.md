### Y1 — Ceiling-residual candidate-account screen (opportunity-cost vs demand-returns), descriptive

1. **First-order link (channel):** **markets** (demand/returns) + **cohort flows** — it screens which candidate
   account can be named for the *unexplained ceiling residual* (why PHL upper-secondary completion never crosses 90
   despite delivery capacity improving; D63/A10).
2. **Discriminating action:** *positive* (opportunity-cost proxies track the ceiling with the predicted sign) →
   the A1 write-up may list "foregone income / opportunity cost" as a *descriptively-supported candidate account* for
   the ceiling residual (still UNABLE as a mechanism, no direction claim). *negative* (null or wrong sign) → the
   write-up reports that account **UNABLE / discarded**, shrinking D63's candidate list to the honest residue.
3. **Why not a niche:** the ceiling residual is the single most-cited open item in the A1 write-up (D63/A10); a
   reader of "why did PHL massify primary/lower-secondary but not upper-secondary" directly cares.
4. **Data on disk:** `09_relations/data/panel_wdi_long.csv` (30 countries): ceiling = terminal `SE.TER.ENRR`
   (tertiary GER, last 3 observed years); candidates = `SL.AGR.EMPL.ZS` (opportunity cost), `SL.UEM.1524.ZS` (youth
   unemployment), `SL.EMP.WORK.ZS` (formal wage-share = returns/demand); income confound removed via rank-partial on
   `NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.CD`. Check (6 systems): `UNIVERSALIZATION_SERIES.csv` `completion_upper_secondary` + `ger_tertiary`
   terminal vs `JOBS_AND_MARKETS_PANEL.csv` `SL.AGR.EMPL.ZS` / `SL.EMP.WORK.ZS`. No DATA ASK.
5. **Threshold + falsifier (fixed):** the opportunity-cost account earns a candidate note **only if** the
   income-partialled Spearman satisfies ρ(ceiling, agri-share) ≤ **−0.40** AND ρ(ceiling, youth-unemp) ≤ **−0.40**;
   **falsifier → UNABLE/discard** if either |ρ| < 0.40 or sign is positive. (A *discard* threshold, never a claim.)
6. **Closed-claim check:** does **not** reopen C1–C4; between-country Spearman = no direction claim (R13-safe); no
   capacity-mechanism claim.
7. **Cost / rank:** **small** (one script, ≤100 lines). Expected information-gain rank **#1**.

### Y2 — Cross-channel lead-lag constraint (schooling ↔ markets), descriptive

1. **First-order link (channel):** the central interaction itself — **schooling and markets "each standing the
   others up"**.
2. **Discriminating action:** *positive* (a visible one-way lead, e.g. schooling leads services/wage-share at 2–3
   lags while the reverse is ≈0) → becomes a **predeclared, target-free descriptive constraint** the model class must
   reproduce in its fit window (enters the write-up as a check, not a fitted schedule). *negative* (null both ways)
   → the write-up states the schooling↔market interaction is **not detectable cross-sectionally**, reinforcing
   EQUIVALENT, and the model is *not* required to reproduce a lead-lag the data do not show.
3. **Why not a niche:** "each standing the others up" is the first-order question verbatim; a reader of the model's
   central interaction cares whether any detectable timing structure exists to constrain it.
4. **Data on disk:** `09_relations/data/panel_wdi_long.csv` (30 countries, 1971–2024): schooling = `SE.SEC.ENRR`,
   `SE.TER.ENRR`; markets = `SL.SRV.EMPL.ZS`, `SL.EMP.WORK.ZS`. Country-demeaned cross-lag partial correlations,
   lags 1–3, both directions, reported as a full matrix. No DATA ASK.
5. **Threshold + falsifier (fixed):** "descriptively visible" **only if** one direction has |partial r| ≥ **0.25** at
   ≥2 consecutive lags with consistent sign **and** the reverse direction is < 0.25; **falsifier → NOT SUPPORTED**
   (report "interaction not detectable", equivalence) otherwise. No causal/direction vocabulary (R13).
6. **Closed-claim check:** R13-safe (lead-lag *description* only, never a push/pull direction or complementarity
   claim — same cap as E5); does not reopen C1–C4.
7. **Cost / rank:** **small**. Rank **#4**.
