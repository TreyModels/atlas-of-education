# BIG-PICTURE RULE — mandatory step-back at every Council cycle

**Standing order from the user, 20 Nov 2026.** The Council must not disappear into a niche thread. At the
start of *every* cycle (and before any new experiment is ordered), the panel performs a **step-back check**
and records it at the top of the synthesis.

## The step-back (write these five lines before anything else)

1. **First-order goal, one sentence.** We are standing up a longitudinal model in which *massification* is
   produced by the interaction of **schooling, government and markets** — each standing the others up — with
   cohort flows and country/sector variation; shadow education is secondary.
2. **Active threads — label each `KEEP`, `PARK`, or `KILL`.** A thread is `KILL` if it cannot change whether
   the model reproduces massification/universalization, or if it is a measurement detail with no path back to
   the model. `PARK` if it is a good question that is not on the current critical path. Be willing to kill.
3. **The one thread that most advances the model this cycle**, and why.
4. **Drift check:** are we measuring instead of modelling? re-fitting a closed claim? adding parameters with
   no discriminating test? chasing a niche that a referee would call irrelevant to massification?
5. **What we would drop if the user said "only one thing matters this week".**

## Where it goes

- Top of every `08_council/brief_0NN/SYNTHESIS_0NN.md`, as `## Step-back (mandatory)`.
- The current keep/park/kill list in `08_council/BIG_PICTURE.md`, updated each cycle.
- A `COUNCIL:` bridge post may be short, but it must state the step-back verdict when it changes the plan.

## Red flags that force a step-back

- The last two Council actions were both about one parameter or one measurement.
- A new experiment is proposed that would not alter the model's ability to produce massification.
- The thread would not be intelligible to a reader of the first-order question.
- We are treating a fitted curve as a "law", a synthetic match as "evidence", or a calibration as "validation".

If two or more red flags are present, the Council must either kill the thread or justify in one paragraph why
it is on the critical path to a model of massification.
