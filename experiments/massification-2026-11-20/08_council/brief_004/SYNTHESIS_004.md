# SYNTHESIS 004 — iteration-2 design review (for `lead`)

**Headline.** The loop's architecture is sound; its sequencing is fit-first. As written, iteration 2 can satisfy
`RESEARCH_LOOP.md` and still be re-parameterisation, because calibration (C) precedes falsification (D) and the
Council gate (E) is last. C1 already shows the danger: on six aggregate moments the two central worlds are
equidistant (0.012–0.023) and an outside-set truth is confidently assigned to the surplus world (2/2). With six
amendments and four added stop rules, the loop becomes a genuine falsification programme; the single variable to
change is **provider supply/entry with teacher-tutor capacity**. `RESEARCH_LOOP.md` appeared at 13:07 (absent at
intake); this review is of that state.

## (a) Is the design sound? Structurally yes, with three sequencing amendments

1. **Gate the design, not just the results.** Council review must occur before calibration (this brief) and after
   (brief 005). A panel that only sees finished runs can audit claims but cannot stop a design from manufacturing
   them.
2. **Label every target row fit or check before the calibration run**, with a predicted sign for each candidate
   world (surplus, Yoo, competitive, outside-set). Targets fitted to all worlds cannot identify any of them.
3. **Predeclare the anti-re-parameterisation tests**, not just the outcomes: the symmetric freeze, the
   truth-outside-the-set case, and the identification-gain metric (§b).

## (b) What iteration 2 must do to avoid mere re-parameterisation — six requirements

1. **New moments with different predictions**, at least two of them real-data and excluded from the fit. Candidates:
   formal-access response to graduate supply (regional campus expansion; PH free tuition 2018); stage-specific
   tutoring take-up with provider composition; wage-premium dynamics against attainment; informality transitions.
2. **Fit/check split and holdout** declared before fitting (period: fit 1980–2010, hold out 2011–2024; at least one
   country or stage untouched). No calibration target may double as a check.
3. **Truth outside the candidate set each round**, with the forced-identification rate reported. C1's G4 is the
   regression test.
4. **At least one behavioural schedule replaced, not recalibrated** (C2's supplied schooling; a generated path must
   be shown stable — B4's endogenous collapse says this is the hard part).
5. **Symmetric two-way test:** freeze schooling (does the job path still rise?) and freeze wages/jobs (does
   attainment still rise?). If either side needs its schedule, name it supplied and remove "generated" from the
   sentence.
6. **Shadow incidence as an outcome:** take-up ranges and provider mix are checks, never fitted shares; fitted
   objects are household demand, supplier entry cost, teacher-tutor capacity. Each cell carries source, date,
   definition, and a range fixed before the model ran.

## (c) Stopping/falsification criteria and stage publishability

Adopt `RESEARCH_LOOP.md` §5 rules 1–5 as written (shadow-incidence failure; two-way imposed; identification failure;
cohort-flow failure; target coded as parameter). **Add four:**
6. **Out-of-sample miss:** held-out error above a predeclared margin ⇒ no country claim.
7. **Unmeasured-parameter sign instability:** if the sign flips over the plausible range of an unestimated parameter
   (the school-support/tutoring substitution elasticity is the live case), withdraw the claim, do not hedge it.
8. **Institutional-parameter audit:** if fitting a country requires country-specific hand-set reader/floor rules,
   label the result a description of those rules, not a mechanism.
9. **Iteration budget:** one structural change per iteration; if two consecutive iterations produce no
   identification gain, stop claiming the mechanism and publish the equivalence/negative map.
**Publishability by stage:** A (country cards) = data descriptor; B (targets) = measurement note; C (calibration) =
model description with residuals, no historical claim; D (falsification) = identification/negative-results paper —
the real product; E (Council) = internal audit, not a publication. The loop as a whole is publishable now as a
methods + negative-map paper. Freeze the stop-rule register with a date/hash before iteration-2 runs.

## (d) The single most important variable to change next

**Provider supply/entry — including teacher-tutor capacity.** Reasons: (i) it is the one structural change that
creates a *new checkable moment* (who supplies the shadow: commercial firms vs teacher-tutors vs informal), which
C1 says is what identification needs; (ii) it endogenizes the economy→education leg (provider entry responds to
demand and capacity), where C2 says the model is one-way; (iii) it addresses the paper's own largest empirical
failure (licensed nurses per migrant 0.25 vs ~9) and the comparative shadow-education record (teacher-tutoring is a
large share in Cambodia/Philippines); (iv) it is observable in country cards (establishments, teacher-tutor share).
Conditional (PATRINOS): if the country cards cannot deliver provider data, the identification claim is `UNABLE TO
ASSESS`; the loop must then report the equivalence result rather than claim a winner. The freeze-both test is the
gate that proves the change was structural, not cosmetic.
