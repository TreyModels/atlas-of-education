## Answers

**1. C10 — fair test?** No. E18 codes support as a universal, free, additive +0.5 sd score module with no
budget constraint, no capacity limit, no homework/teacher-time channel and no unpaid help. Under rank gates, a
universal score boost crowds takers toward the cut-offs, so paid spend rises at the apex (4092 → 4815; apex share
0.411 → 0.675) while college entry Q3–5 *falls* (0.372 → 0.312). That is a real **model-class** insight ("the race
absorbs the subsidy") but not evidence about China, where the 2021 bundle combined a for-profit ban, homework
reduction and in-school support. The one arm resembling the policy — partial access, A5 — *does* reduce paid spend
(3855 vs baseline 4092, −236.5), which the brief does not notice. Minimal alternative specification: (a) make support
and tutoring imperfect substitutes in the score function (CES, elasticity > 0); (b) target support at low performers;
(c) make the household budget bind; (d) add a teacher-tutor channel. **Run it:** E18b, 3–4 arms, ~90–180 s total;
predeclared threshold: support+ban paid spend below ban-only by > 2 seed sd. If substitution appears, C10 is
withdrawn; if it does not, the "race absorbs the subsidy" result becomes defensible as a model-class claim.

**2. C17 — what may be claimed?** The income framing dies: the matched rival reproduces zero/negative diploma-income
changes at σ ≤ 1 (E13b: −0.059 at σ=0.5; −0.034/−0.031 at σ=1.0; surplus −0.061/−0.042). Two caveats belong in the
same sentence: the σ=0.5 estimate is −0.059 ± 0.059 and σ=1.0 is ≈1.6 sd from zero, so a 2-sd rule would not reject
zero; and the worlds are not level-matched (baseline formal share 0.286 vs 0.463; rival degree income 32.3 ± 23.3 vs
2.93). The formal-probability fall (−0.154 vs ≈ +0.02) survives only as: "in a model where hiring is
credential-rationed, a graduate-supply shock with fixed vacancies lowers diploma holders' formal-employment
probability substantially more than under productivity hiring at σ ≤ 2." Not "Prediction 2 supported". Prediction 2
must be rewritten: drop absolute income, keep formal-employment probability, add a level-matched benchmark arm
(E13c) and predeclare the half-the-gap rule.

**3. E3/E5/E7 — discovery vs mechanical.** E3: the collapse (Q1 0.462 → 0.017) is the coded cash constraint; the
*safe* headline is the multiplicative shock × credit interaction (0.212 with no credit vs 0.714 with credit), still
model-conditional. E5: the monotone memory response is a property of the smoothing rule plus the coded return path —
report as sensitivity ("memory horizon is a first-order modelling choice"), not as a claim about families. E7: the
sweep is Yoo; the residual is the ban/leak accounting (all-formal ban leaves 88% of spend at leak=0.5) — but the leak
is an assumption, so present it as a scenario, not a finding. None of the three should be headlined.

**4. E7 residual novel claim.** "Under a 50% informal substitute market, banning formal gate tutoring re-routes
spend rather than deleting demand (88% persists); enforcement and leaks, not gate design, determine incidence."
Conditional scenario, consistent with the comparative record (Korea 1980, China 2021). Cite Yoo for the sweep.

**5. C19 and four-country claims.** Disqualified: uncalibrated defaults miss their own targets (basic completion
0.739 vs 0.55–0.62; basic→college 0.227 vs ~0.70; GPI 0.868 vs 1.2–1.3). Minimum disclosure: target series and
vintage; fitted versus uncalibrated parameters; fit criteria and misses; pilot flag for E19/E20; HK data-blocked; the
transfer "NULL" is model-to-model, not evidence. No four-country claim in this report.

**6. Lead claim and withdrawals.** Lead with: *"In this model class, a graduate-supply shock with formal vacancies
fixed lowers diploma holders' formal-employment probability substantially under credential-rationed hiring; the
absolute-income version of the claim fails, and the competitive benchmark shows no comparable fall at σ ≤ 2."*
Second plank: the floor/race decomposition (floor = eligibility; ranking = intensification; the floor's own
persistence contribution is ≤ noise). **Withdraw:** C3/C16 income discriminator; C14 sweep as a discovery; C10 as a
Double Reduction test; C12/C13 as discoveries; any four-country/HK/transfer claim; "floor engine of persistence".
Keep E2 accounting validation; keep C10 as a model-class positional-absorption result once E18b runs.
