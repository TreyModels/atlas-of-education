## Agreed findings

1. **C10 is not a test of Double Reduction.** E18's support arm is a universal, free, additive +0.5 sd score module
   with no demand channel, no budget constraint and no capacity limit. The observed rise in paid spend (4092 → 4815;
   apex share 0.411 → 0.675) is a positional-race implication *within the model class*, not evidence about China. A
   fair test needs a targeted/substitution specification (E18b), which is cheap (~90 s per E18 re-run).
2. **C17 kills the income discriminator, not Prediction 2 wholesale.** The matched rival produces zero/negative
   diploma income changes at σ ≤ 1 (E13b: −0.059 at σ=0.5; −0.034/−0.031 at σ=1.0; surplus −0.061/−0.042). But
   the σ=0.5 estimate is −0.059 ± 0.059 and the σ=1.0 estimates are ≈1.6 sd from zero; the worlds are not
   level-matched (baseline formal share 0.286 vs 0.463; rival degree income 32.3 ± 23.3 vs 2.93). The formal-
   probability discriminator (−0.154 vs ≈+0.02) survives as a *model-class* conditional, not as a measurement.
3. **C12/C13 are near-mechanical.** Hard cash constraint ⇒ Q1 collapse; smoothing rule + coded return path ⇒ memory
   effect. Their magnitudes are calibration outcomes; report as sensitivity, not discovery.
4. **C14's sweep is Yoo.** Residual novel content is the ban/leak accounting (spend re-routes; all-formal ban leaves
   88% under leak=0.5), which is assumption-driven and should be presented as a scenario.
5. **C19 disqualifies four-country claims.** Uncalibrated defaults miss their own stated targets (0.739 vs
   0.55–0.62; 0.227 vs ~0.70; GPI 0.868 vs 1.2–1.3); HK is data-blocked; transfer is model-to-model. No country claim.

## Disagreements preserved

**D1. PAGE vs BRAY — artifact or insight?**
PAGE: the complementarity is generated by the rank structure, so "the race absorbs the subsidy" is a genuine
model-class result, not a pure artifact of an additive function. BRAY: for the policy question the model is
mis-specified (no unpaid help, no teacher-tutors, basic-gate share 0.040 is implausible for Cambodia/Philippines), so
the result cannot travel to Double Reduction. *What turns on it:* whether E18's result is reported as mechanism or as
a specification caveat. *Resolution:* report both; the mechanism is real *in the model class*; the policy test is not
run. *Settles it:* E18b with substitution, targeting and a teacher-tutor channel.

**D2. PATRINOS vs CARTWRIGHT — "contradicted" or "not robust"?**

verdict is "the income discriminator fails", not "the theory is refuted". CARTWRIGHT: the predeclared rule is a
decision rule for the paper, not a significance test; both statements must appear, with the 2-sd caveat. *Resolution:*
C3/C16 marked CONTRADICTED *as an income discriminator*, with the precision/level caveats in the same sentence.

**D3. Everyone — E13b's "matched" worlds are not level-matched.**

comparison is a compare of responses from different equilibria. The formal-probability discriminator needs E13c
(equalize baseline formal share and income scale, then shock). PATRINOS will not accept a claim on the current cell;
others concur.
