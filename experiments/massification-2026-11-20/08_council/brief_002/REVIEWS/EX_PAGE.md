# REVIEW — PAGE composite (drawing on Page 2018; Miller & Page 2007) — brief_002

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: *The Model Thinker* (2018) and *Complex Adaptive Systems* (2007) — models as
reasoning instruments, results as properties of model classes unless shown otherwise. **COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:**
the score-function assessment below is the composite's, not a published claim.

**M. MODEL RENDERING.** Take the score production function the model must be using: exam score `S = f(support) +
g(tutoring) + ε`, gates allocate by {fixed bar, rank quota}, and tutoring is chosen by expected marginal effect on
passing. Under additive `f+g`, universal free support shifts the whole distribution up but leaves the *distance from
each taker to the cut-off* unchanged; with rank allocation the pass line moves up with the distribution, so the race
re-forms at the apex. Substitution would require `∂²S/∂support∂tutoring < 0` (diminishing joint returns) **and** a
binding budget or need constraint. Prediction: with those coded, support-only must raise positional spend at the
highest-stakes gate and cannot reduce it. That is what E18 shows: spend 4092 → 4815, apex share 0.411 → 0.675,
college Q3–5 *falls* 0.372 → 0.312, completion +0.008.

**A. VERDICTS.** C10 **SIMULATION-ONLY, specification-dependent**: the result is entailed by (i) additive score
production, (ii) universal (not targeted) support, (iii) no budget constraint to relieve, (iv) no capacity limit, (v)
no demand channel. It is *not* a fair test of Trey's Double Reduction hypothesis, which bundled a for-profit ban,
homework reduction and in-school support. It *is* a fair demonstration of a model-class result: a free score subsidy
without demand relief is absorbed by the positional race at apex gates. C11 **SIMULATION-ONLY, assumption-driven**
(leak=0.5 codes the informal substitute market; the −63% is a leak=0 result). C12/C13 **near-mechanical** (see
DELIBERATION). C14 **mechanical** (bar sweep = Yoo). C16 **superseded by C17**. C17/C18/C19 → other reviewers.

**B. WHAT IS IMPOSED.** The support-without-substitution result is not merely an additive-function artifact — the
rank structure alone re-forms competition at the apex, so even a production function with some substitution may
produce the sign, depending on the elasticity. But the *size* (apex share 0.68) and the failure to substitute are
coded, because support is universal, free, and demand-side inert. The minimal alternative specification: one
substitution parameter in the score function (CES between support and tutoring, elasticity > 0) plus a targeted
support arm (bottom quintile) plus a budget-binding arm. Three arms, one re-run of E18, ~90 s.

**C. ESTIMAND.** "Paid tutoring spend" is a model quantity; "support worth 0.5 score sd" is an input, not a measured
policy. No observed Double Reduction magnitudes are in the batch.

**D. TYPE CONFUSION.** C10 is correctly labelled simulation. The danger is prose: "school support intensifies
positional spending" is a model-class claim, not a finding about China.

**E. STRONGEST DEFENSIBLE SENTENCE.** "In a model where support and tutoring add to the same exam score and support
is universal and free, a school-support subsidy raises paid tutoring spend at the highest-stakes gate and leaves
completion roughly unchanged; substitution does not appear because the coded household faces no budget or need
constraint and support is untargeted."

**F. STRONGEST NEGATIVE RESULT.** Support-only *raises* completion (+0.008) while *lowering* Q3–5 college entry
(0.372 → 0.312): the subsidy helps the marginal student but intensifies the positional contest. This is worth
reporting as a model insight; it is not noticed as such in the brief.

**G2. STEEL-MAN.** The rival reading is positional-race absorption (Hirsch/Collins literature): subsidies to a
positional good are dissipated in competition. The batch supports it *within the model*; it does not measure it.

**H. ALTERNATIVE.** A demand-side model: school support reduces the marginal return to tutoring (same content,
teacher feedback), households spend less. Not coded; runnable.

**I. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND.** E18b with substitution and targeting; an observed Chinese tutoring-spend series
before/after 2021 with the ban and support introduced separately. Neither is in the batch.
