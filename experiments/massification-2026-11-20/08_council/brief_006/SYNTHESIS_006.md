## 1. Confirmation and precise scope

Rule 1 was predeclared: ≥2 of 4 main countries outside the literature range after calibration, with no plausible
parameter fix. S1: assessable HKG/KHM/PHL **3/3 outside** (PHL all-basic 23.5 vs 46.5; KHM G9 21.3 vs 68.4, primary
12.8 vs 41.3, urban 19.6 vs 60.5, rural 10.5 vs 26.4; HKG lower 30.6 vs 72, middle 40.2 vs 82; PHL upper 39.4 vs
28.1 over-predicts). HKG gradient: 2 misses. KOR/JPN and HKG-1996: UNABLE, not counted. S2: no defensible structural
reproduction for PHL (baseline 15.2 → joint 11.7 pp RMSE; one-parameter gains ≤0.6 pp); KHM 8.3 pp is a boundary fit;
HKG 0.2 pp uses 8 parameters for 2 targets (saturation, not replication). S3: UNABLE — only Korean fee moments exist
and there is no KOR variant. **Stop rule 1 fired.**

**Withdrawn (precise):** (1) shadow-education incidence, any form; (2) the HK 0.85 "match" as evidence — it is the
calibration target, and counted as a check it fails at 54.4 vs 85; (3) loop-2's near-universal take-up as a result;
(4) "structural parameters can generate non-universal tutoring across countries"; (5) S2's HKG fit as replication;
(6) S3's price/entry block as calibrated economics.
**Not withdrawn:** asymmetric-feedback negative (job side 3–4% generated; economy→education genuine; 20–40% shadow
residual, upper bound); floor/race decomposition with the mixed-knockout caveat; the brief-003 graduate-supply
formal-access conditional; the method/negative map; the restatement audit; provider-entry *direction*; the country
cards and target register. The failure is bidirectional across stages, which indicates a missing structural channel
(provider composition/school quality), not a parameter.

## 3. The single rescue experiment — Korea no-fit benchmark

Build a KOR variant with structural parameters **frozen** from the uncalibrated baseline (no take-up fitting; fee
parameters may be fitted only to Korean fee moments), predeclare the official 2023 stage rates (86.0 / 75.4 / 66.4;
all 78.5), and compare stage-matched take-up. **Threshold:** all reported stages within ±5 pp and provider/hours
direction consistent ⇒ a **one-country** incidence claim for Korea is restored; otherwise the stop-rule stands
permanently. **Cost ~30–45 min.** Passing does not rescue massification incidence; KHM/PH transfer stays an
out-of-sample test expected to fail and must be reported as such. This is the cheapest decisive use of the only
official annual national series the programme has.
