## Disagreements preserved

**D1. BRAY vs PAGE — is the target data too weak to have tested the model at all?**
BRAY: HKG is second-hand, PH a select 2010 sample, KHM a six-school case; the check is a check against weak targets.
PAGE: weak targets cut both ways — they cannot validate *or* cleanly falsify a general incidence claim; the stop rule
is nevertheless the right response because the programme cannot currently do better. *Resolution:* the withdrawal
stands (it is a measurement finding as much as a model finding), and the measurement programme becomes the product.

**D2. PATRINOS vs CARTWRIGHT — scope of the withdrawal.**
PATRINOS: withdraw only incidence; do not let the failure stain the formal-access conditional or the negative map.
CARTWRIGHT: same, and add the unfalsifiability guard: no later re-tuning may be called a match; only a predeclared
no-fit external benchmark can reopen the claim. *Resolution:* adopted verbatim in the sign-off.

**D3. Which rescue experiment is the single one?**
BRAY: a data-first national series would be the real rescue, but it is not an experiment the fleet can run today.
PATRINOS: the KOR no-fit benchmark (structural parameters frozen; official 2023 stage rates; fee parameters may use
Korean fee moments only; ±5 pp at all stages) — cheap and decisive. PAGE: concur; passing restores a one-country
claim, not general incidence. *Resolution:* KOR no-fit benchmark is the single rescue experiment, explicitly
one-country.
