# REVIEW — CARTWRIGHT composite (drawing on Cartwright 1999; Cartwright & Hardie 2012) — brief_006

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: stop rules are pre-commitments; when a predeclared rule fires, the honest act is
to withdraw the claim and keep the record, not to re-specify until it passes; capacities are local and the scope
conditions must be stated with the claim. **COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the adjudication and drafting below are the
composite's.

**M. MODEL RENDERING.** M1 the stop rule was declared before loop-3 results, with a quantitative threshold and an
"and no plausible parameter change fixes it" clause. M2 S1 supplies the threshold evidence (3/3 outside; HKG gradient
2). M3 S2 supplies the second clause (PH best 11.7 pp; one-parameter effects ≤0.6 pp). M4 the event is therefore
properly adjudicated. M5 the withdrawal must be recorded in the report and loop documents with the evidence and a date,
not just announced. M6 the claim can be re-opened only by a predeclared test on data not used in the fit.

**A. VERDICT — THE STOP-RULE EVENT IS CONFIRMED AND VALIDLY FIRED.** The rule was predeclared, the test was fair
(calibration cell excluded; UNABLE rows marked, not counted), the threshold was met, and the "no plausible parameter
fix" clause is satisfied by S2. This is the programme's cleanest methodological success: it predicted its own failure
condition and then met it.

**B. WITHDRAWN vs RETAINED (precise).**
*Withdrawn:* (1) shadow-education incidence — any statement that the model reproduces the literature's take-up by
stage/provider/country; (2) the HK 0.85 "match" as evidence (calibration target; excluded); (3) loop-2's
near-universal take-up as a result; (4) "structural parameters generate non-universal tutoring" (S2); (5) S2's HKG
fit as replication (8 params/2 targets); (6) S3's price/entry block as calibrated economics (UNABLE).
*Retained:* the asymmetric-feedback negative; the floor/race decomposition (with its mixed-knockout caveat); the
graduate-supply formal-access conditional (brief 003); the method and negative map; the restatement audit; the
provider-entry *direction*; S3's diagnostic that the loop-2 floor collapse was an under-priced entry block; the
country cards and target register.
**No further withdrawal is required.** The stop rule is about incidence; extending it to the whole programme would be
over-withdrawal and would misstate the record.

**E. THE UNFALSIFIABILITY GUARD.** Because S2 shows no structural parameter can bring PHL to 46.5, no later
re-tuning of the same specification may be presented as "now the model matches". The only permissible route back to an
incidence claim is a predeclared, no-fit external benchmark — the KOR variant experiment. Say this in the report.

**F. NEGATIVE.** The programme's one-second-hand, caveated HK number was the only rate ever used to fit, and the
regime that produced it is the same one whose authors called the survey "not statistically robust". That should be
stated where the withdrawal is stated.

**G2. STEEL-MAN.** A reader may say the model was never meant to be validated on these targets. Then the report must
say that too — but it also must not cite the model as evidence for incidence anywhere, including in the October
paper's claims about shadow education.

**H. ALTERNATIVE.** Treat the failure as the finding: incidence is not identifiable with the available targets; the
publishable object is the measurement audit plus the negative map.

**I. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND.** The KOR no-fit benchmark passing on a predeclared range, or nationally
representative stage/provider series for at least two countries.
