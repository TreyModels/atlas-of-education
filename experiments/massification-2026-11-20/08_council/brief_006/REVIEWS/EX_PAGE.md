# REVIEW — PAGE composite (drawing on Page 2018; Miller & Page 2007) — brief_006

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: a model class is only as useful as the contrasts it can support; when a check
fails in both directions across stages, the structure, not the parameterisation, is implicated; publish what the
model class can now support, not what it was meant to support. **COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the ranking below is the
composite's.

**M. MODEL RENDERING.** M1 the programme now has a model class with: rank-ordered hiring/slot rationing (formal-access
conditional), a one-way economy (job side schedule-like), a shadow sector with provider entry but uncalibrated prices,
and an information/reader block. M2 its publishable claims are the ones that survive within-class testing and have a
stated scope condition. M3 what it cannot support: cross-country incidence levels, two-way feedback as a general
claim, trajectories. M4 the negative map is a legitimate product: it tells the field which mechanisms are
observationally equivalent on available national aggregates.

**A. VERDICTS.** **Stop-rule event confirmed; incidence withdrawn.** From a model-class standpoint this is a clean
falsification of the shadow-incidence hypothesis *within the class*: the failure is bidirectional across stages (loop 2
universal, loop 3 under-predicts most rows, PHL upper over-predicts), which is characteristic of a missing structural
channel (school-quality/supplier composition), not of a bad parameter value. That is a more useful finding than a fit.

**B. WHAT THE MODEL CLASS CAN NOW SUPPORT.** (i) The graduate-supply formal-access conditional (brief 003, one
level-matched cell, simulation-only); (ii) the floor/race decomposition (model-class, with the mixed-knockout caveat);
(iii) the asymmetric-feedback negative (education→economy 3–4%, economy→education genuine, 20–40% shadow residual
upper bound); (iv) the provider-entry *direction* (uncalibrated magnitude); (v) the identification/negative map
(C1-style no-ID, forced-ID). Nothing about incidence, trajectories, or country levels.

**C. PUBLISHABLE OBJECTS — ranking.** **1. Combination (a) method + negative/identification map + (c) data-first
measurement programme, as one paper.** It is the only object fully supported by executed cells and requiring no new
data or fits: predeclared checks, stop-rule execution, restatement audit, no-ID/forced-ID, asymmetric feedback, and a
21-row shadow target register with provenance. **2. (c) standalone measurement programme.** The six country cards and
the target register are a real contribution to comparative measurement (definitions, denominators, provenance,
NOT-FOUND flags); publishable on its own, and it is the necessary precondition for anyone (including this programme) to
test incidence later. **3. (b) provider/fee economics on Korea, conditional.** Only if loop 4 delivers the KOR variant
and a defensible fee fit: the Korean official survey (participation 78.5%, 434k won/month per student, 553k per
participant, 7.3 h/week, 27.1tn total) is the one place where a provider-market paper could stand. Without the fee
fit it is one more stylized simulation. Do not rank it above the measurement programme.

**D. TYPE.** S1 is a test of the model's descriptive capability; its failure is a *negative result about the model
class*, not a null about the world. Say both.

**E. STRONGEST DEFENSIBLE SENTENCE.** "On predeclared stage-matched checks the structural shadow model does not
reproduce the literature's take-up in any assessable country, in both directions across stages; the programme
therefore reports the failure, the data limits, and the identification map rather than an incidence claim."

**F. NEGATIVE.** The most valuable single result in the loop may be S3's structural note: loop-2's price collapse was
an under-priced entry-cost block, not an intrinsic market result. That is a genuine model-class discovery about the
provider block — small, but real.

**G2. STEEL-MAN.** A defender could say: the targets are weak, so failing them means little. True — and it is exactly
why the measurement programme is the right product: the field cannot test incidence claims until the targets are
better.

**H. ALTERNATIVE.** The programme could publish as a methods paper with the negative map and a data agenda, and defer
any substantive claim about massification to the next iteration with real moments.

**I. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND.** A KOR no-fit benchmark that passes; real-data moments that separate the worlds;
a provider-fee calibration with observed moments.
