# REVIEW — PATRINOS composite (drawing on Psacharopoulos & Patrinos 2018; Becker 1964; steel-man seat) — brief_006

**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: a calibration that uses a moment as its target cannot also be evidence for it; a
fit with more parameters than targets is saturation; identification needs independent moments and a holdout.
**COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the audit below is the composite's.

**M. MODEL RENDERING.** M1 S1 is a legitimate out-of-sample check: structural parameters were not fitted to the
stage-matched rows (loop-3 baseline), the calibration cell (HK 0.85) is excluded, KOR/JPN marked UNABLE. M2 S2
searched 8 structural parameters against 2–4 targets per country. M3 A "reproduction" requires more targets than
parameters or an independent validation set. M4 A rescue must be a predeclared, no-fit benchmark on data the model has
never seen.

**A. VERDICTS.** **S1: fair, and it fails.** 3/3 assessable countries outside, HKG gradient 2 misses; the threshold
fires exactly as predeclared. **S2: the honest reading is "no defensible structural reproduction"** — PHL best joint
RMSE 11.7 pp (baseline 15.2) with one-parameter improvements of at most +0.6 pp: the structural parameters are simply
not the levers. KHM 8.3 pp is "partial" only by the loop's ±10 pp label, and the joint solution sits at parameter
bounds (pub = 0.0, stake 2.44, inc 2.87) — a boundary fit is not evidence. HKG's 0.2 pp with **8 parameters for 2
targets** is saturation and must not be reported as replication. **S3: UNABLE, correctly labelled** — only Korean fee
moments exist and there is no KOR variant; the structural note (floor collapse = under-priced entry block) is a model
diagnostic, not a calibration. **The stop-rule event is confirmed on its predeclared terms.**

**B. WHAT IS WITHDRAWN (precise).** W1: any claim that the model reproduces shadow-education incidence by
stage/provider/country. W2: the HK 0.85 "match" as evidence (it is the calibration target; if counted as a check it
fails at 54.4 vs 85). W3: loop-2's near-universal take-up as a result (one-intercept artifact). W4: "structural
parameters can generate non-universal tutoring" (S2 PHL failure). W5: S2's HKG "reproduces" as validation. Not
withdrawn: the asymmetric-feedback negative, provider entry as an uncalibrated mechanism direction, the negative map,
the restatement audit, and the independent brief-003 graduate-supply formal-access conditional.

**C. DATA REALITY.** No country has a nationally representative, unambiguous, stage-matched tutoring rate: KOR has an
official survey but no model variant; HKG's 85 is second-hand and the 1996 series is low-robustness; JPN is full-text
but the series is disputed; PHL is one select sample; KHM is a two-school case; CHN is unresolved. Any "incidence"
claim is therefore untestable with current targets, which is why the withdrawal is a measurement finding as much as a
model finding.

**D. TYPES.** S1 is a *check*, not an estimate; S2 is a structural search, not identification; S3 is UNABLE. Do not let
the summary table label any of them "reproduces".

**E. THE SINGLE RESCUE EXPERIMENT — Korea no-fit benchmark.** Build a KOR variant whose structural parameters are
frozen from the uncalibrated baseline (no take-up fitting; fee parameters may be fitted only to the official Korean fee
moments), predeclare the official 2023 stage gradient (78.5% all levels; school-level series from the MODS survey), and
compare stage-matched take-up. Threshold: all reported stages within ±5 pp and provider/hours direction consistent ⇒
a **one-country** incidence claim for Korea is restored; otherwise the stop-rule stands permanently for the
programme's shadow-incidence claims. Cost ~30–45 min (config + official-survey extraction; model run is seconds).
Caveat: passing would rescue a Korea-specific claim, not massification incidence; transfer to KHM/PH stays an
out-of-sample test that will likely fail and must be reported as such.

**F. NEGATIVE.** The only verified, official, annually collected shadow-education series in the set is Korean, and the
programme has not yet built the variant that could use it — the cheapest high-value data the programme is leaving on
the table.

**G2. STEEL-MAN.** A defender may argue S2's joint fit at 8.3 pp for KHM shows the structure can get close. But a
four-target fit with eight parameters at bounds is exactly what "weak identification" means; it cannot carry a claim.

**H. ALTERNATIVE.** A genuinely measurement-first programme could treat the 21-row register as the object: report what
is verifiable, by stage and provider, and stop trying to make the model match soft targets.

**I. WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND.** A KOR no-fit benchmark within ±5 pp at all stages, or an independent stage-matched
survey for PH/KHM with a predeclared range.
