**G. GROUNDING.** Literature basis: a paper that explains persistence, policy failure and success has no falsifier; a

**COUNCIL EXTRAPOLATION:** the audit below is the composite's.

**A. KOREA AND THE TWO-MARGIN POINT.** The paper's Korea numbers must be replaced with the on-disk official 2023
figures: 78.5% all levels; 86.0 elementary / 75.4 middle / 66.4 high; spend per student 398/449/491k won (+23.4%
low-to-high); 553k per participant. v2's "over 80%" is true only for elementary. The substantive problem is bigger:
Korea's participation falls with stage while HKG 2003 rises (35.1/46.6/70.3). The paper's Regime II mechanism
(terminal-gate arms race) predicts HKG's shape, not Korea's. The honest move is to add the **two-margin structure**
(extensive participation × intensive hours/spend) and state that no one-margin gate story can hold both — which is
exactly brief 009's predeclared impossibility for the synthetic families. HKG (with the 2003 caveat) belongs in the
typology; Korea's cell label needs revision or a two-margin mechanism.

**B. CITATION AUDIT (flagged, not asserted).** Concrete problems: **Banerjee, Cole, Duflo & Linden (2007)** is a
remedial-education RCT (balsakhi) with *positive* learning effects; the paper uses it for "minimal academic gains from
private tutoring" — that is a misattribution and a referee will catch it. **Liu & Bray (2020)** is cited in §1 for
India/coaching bans, but the reference is their IJED paper on Ethiopia/Kenya/Tanzania. Unverified in this session:
Gupta (2021, CER), Agarwal (2020, Sage), Whitaker (2010, LAP), Zhang & Liu (2022), "40% of household education
budgets", "300,000 instructors", Taiwan 26→83%. None may be asserted as verified; all need source checks.

**C. MISSING RIVALS.** The paper does not engage Thurow's job-competition queue, Spence/Arrow signalling, Hirsch's
positional goods, or Bray's own shadow-education framework as the primary rival. Without that, "overflow governance"
is not distinguished from a re-description of credential competition. It also doesn't confront the Colombia/Perry
evidence that much tutoring has weak learning effects for a different reason (pedagogy), which is a rival mechanism.

**D. PUBLISHABLE.** As-is: no. Ranking: (1) the **measurement paper** from this programme (real data, KOR/HKG
anomaly, six country cards, parameter map); (2) the theory paper **after** operationalisation and the rival
engagement; (3) the Cambodia case (narrow but solid). The theory paper cannot lead because its evidence base is
thinner than its claims.
