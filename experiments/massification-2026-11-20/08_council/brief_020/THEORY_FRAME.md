# THEORY FRAME — the candidate micro-frame for the massification write-up and the synthetic model

> **One page. Status: CANDIDATE THEORY FRAME — not evidence, not the macro-model.** Combines the two working papers
> (both the programme PI's drafts) into a single account of *what schooling installs* and *why it persists*. Every
> numbered claim below is **theory-only** unless explicitly marked **declared-assumption** (encoded in the synthetic
> generator) or **on-disk descriptive** (already measured). Nothing here may be cited as established evidence (D49).

## The frame in one paragraph

Mass schooling is a **scaffold** whose forward adoption is a local gain (literacy unlocks the first industrial step;
credentials give employers a cheap screen), but whose installation **transforms the state** from which exit must later
be attempted — complementary capital, cohort demography, institutional rules and the distribution of competence all
reorganize around it (hysteresis paper: C_M/C_D/C_E/C_K). The specific thing the scaffold installs into each person is
**not a curriculum but an induced competence**: literacy, numeracy and schedule-discipline are assembled *within
development* on a conserved architecture by repeated exposure to the practice — they have **no ancestral counterpart as
a coordinated whole**, even though their components (number sense, executive control, sequential rule learning) are
ancient (assembly paper: induction, §3.2). The two papers therefore meet at the **educational credential**: an
*induced competence* (installed by schooling) whose *exit* is asymmetric (the credential persists after its value
decays because employers screen, applicants acquire, schools expand and regulators gate). The programme's own object —
the PHL system whose **floor and middle crossed 90 but whose upper-secondary ceiling never did** — is the junction: the
floor is induction (literacy universalized), the ceiling is exit-cost (a credential gate that cannot be left).

## The exact theory-only claims (do NOT encode, do NOT cite as evidence)

1. **Release** — that height, pubertal timing and masticatory morphology moved because a *constraint was removed*, to an
   independently estimable ancestral reference (assembly §3.1). *Theory-only; no anthropometric data on disk.*
2. **The extended developmental window to ~32** (Mousley et al. 2025) being *occupied* by administered input, and the
   stronger claim that jurisdiction *lengthened* it. *The paper itself sets the stronger reading aside (§4); both are
   citation-only here.*
3. **The tracker-vs-physicist agency ranking** — self-generated option construction occupies a larger share of the
   tracker's day (assembly §5). *Explicitly a prediction, not a finding; no observation data.*
4. **Genetic sameness as a designed inference** (assembly §7) — the transplant/reform/migration designs establishing
   that installation dominates ancestry. *Conceptual; we hold no adoption/migrant/reform panel.*
5. **Relational self-domestication** — "an increasing proportion of the viable phenotype is specified by conditions the
   population's own constructed niche produces and maintains" (assembly §1/§9). *A frame, not a measurement; no on-disk
   proxy for "niche-specified phenotype share".*
6. **§8 Predictions 1, 3, 4** (release→ancestral reference; reform→behavioural; installation→dominates ancestry) and
   **hysteresis Predictions 2, 3** (dependency topology→withdrawal loss; return costs decomposable). *All theory-only;
   not reachable from a 30-country expansion-era panel.*
7. **Every micro citation the papers marshal** (Dehaene–Cohen neuronal recycling; Brinch–Galloway; Judd; Lochner–Moretti;
   Zacharopoulos; Corruccini–Beecher; Mousley). *External, not re-verified; enter the write-up only via the verified
   external-review rows (Leuven–Oosterbeek ~45%; returns ~9%; PHL 39% overeducated), never as our own findings.*

## The gating rule (what must happen before any of this becomes more than vocabulary)

- **DT1 AND DT2** (induction dose-response; release-vs-induction discriminating test) must both return **non-null** before
  the generator may *name* the induction mechanism in its declared-assumption provenance. If either is null, the
  induction grounding is **PARKED** and the two assumptions keep their current "declared" label with no theory citation.
- **No new parameter, no new layer, no "induction/hysteresis/window" term** — the D81 lesson stands (a named parameter
  does not buy the mechanism; toy A1 showed a fixed cost does not buy hysteresis).
- **A DT PASS is descriptive only** — it never licenses "the model reproduces induction/hysteresis," and never reopens
  C1–C4 or the closed ceiling residual.

## What this frame is FOR, and what it is not

**For:** naming the mechanism behind two assumptions the synthetic generator already makes, so the write-up can say *why*
literacy is a threshold and *why* schooling formats persons — instead of leaving them as unstated priors. **Not for:**
generating the scaling laws, the crossing years, or the timing (the frame is silent on forward dynamics); explaining the
PHL ceiling (it stays UNABLE until tested); or replacing the M+V re-specification / v1.2 work, which remain the critical
path (D96–D97, §3w).
