Private Education Expenditures Survey of Elementary, 
Middle and High School Students in 2023
The total private education expenditures of elementary, middle and high school 
students recorded 27.1 trillion won in 2023, which rose by 1.2 trillion won (4.5%) 
from 2022.
○ As for the total private education expenditures by school level, elementary school students 
recorded 12.4 trillion won in 2023, which rose by 4.3% from 2022. Middle school students 
recorded 7.2 trillion won in 2023, which rose by 1.0% from 2022. High school students 
recorded 7.5 trillion won in 2023, which rose by 8.2% from 2022.
* Change in the number of students: 5.28 million persons (2022) → 5.21 million persons 
<a drop of 70 thousand persons (1.3%)>
The private education participation rate stood at 78.5% in 2023, up 0.2%p from 
2022. The weekly participation hours marked 7.3 hours per student in 2023, 
which grew by 0.1 hour from 2022.
○ As for the private education participation rate by school level, elementary school students 
stood at 86.0% in 2023, up 0.8%p from 2022. Middle school students stood at 75.4% 
in 2023, down 0.8%p from 2022. High school students stood at 66.4% in 2023, up 0.5%p 
from 2022.
○ As for the private education participation hours by school level, elementary school 
students marked 7.5 hours in 2023, rising by 0.1 hour from 2022. Middle school students 
marked 7.4 hours in 2023, falling by 0.1 hour from 2022. High school students marked 
6.7 hours in 2023, rising by 0.1 hour from 2022.
The average monthly private education expenditures per student recorded 434 
thousand won in 2023, rising by 5.8% from 2022. The average monthly private 
education expenditures per participation student recorded 553 thousand won in 
2023, rising by 5.5% from 2022.
○ (Total students)
The average monthly private education expenditures per elementary school student recorded 
398 thousand won (25 thousand won, 6.8%↑). The average monthly private education 
expenditures per middle school student recorded 449 thousand won (12 thousand won, 2.6%
↑). The average monthly private education expenditures per high school student recorded 
491 thousand won (32 thousand won, 6.9%↑).
* General subjects: 326 thousand won <a rise of 16 thousand won (5.3%↑)>, Artistic & 
physical skills, hobbies and culture: 105 thousand won <a rise of 7 thousand won (7.1%↑)>
○ (Participation students)
The average monthly private education expenditures per elementary school participation 
student marked 462 thousand won (25 thousand won, 5.7%↑). The average monthly private 
education expenditures per middle school participation student marked 596 thousand won 
(21 thousand won, 3.7%↑). The average monthly private education expenditures per high 
school participation student marked 740 thousand won (43 thousand won, 6.1%↑).
* General subjects: 518 thousand won <a rise of 28 thousand won (5.7%↑)>, Artistic & 
physical skills, hobbies and culture: 226 thousand won <a rise of 10 thousand won (4.7%↑)>
The average monthly private education expenditures on general subjects amounted 
to 326 thousand won per student in 2023, rising by 5.3% from 2022. The average 
monthly private education expenditures on general subjects amounted to 518 
thousand won per participation student in 2023, rising by 5.7% from 2022.
○ (Total students)
The average monthly private education expenditures on English recorded the highest figure 
of 128 thousand won per student, which was followed by mathematics (122 thousand won), 
Korean (38 thousand won) and 'Social studies and science' (19 thousand won).
- Compared to 2022, the average monthly private education expenditures on Korean (11.1%), 
'Social studies and science' (8.2%), mathematics (5.6%) and English (3.8%) recorded an 
increase.
○ (Participation students)
The average monthly private education expenditures on English recorded the highest figure of 
248 thousand won per participation student, which was followed by mathematics (233 thousand 
won), Korean (148 thousand won) and 'Social studies and science' (137 thousand won).
- Compared to 2022, the average monthly private education expenditures on Korean (8.2%), 
'Social studies and science' (7.4%), mathematics (5.9%) and English (5.0%) recorded an 
increase.
The higher household income level, the higher education expenditures.
○ As for the average monthly private education expenditures, the households earning 8 
million won or more recorded the highest figure of 671 thousand won per student. 
Whereas, the households earning less than 3 million won recorded 183 thousand won 
per student.
- Compared to 2022, the average monthly private education expenditures of the households 
earning 8 million won or more rose by 3.5%. The average monthly private education 
expenditures of the households earning less than 3 million won rose by 3.0%.
'Dual income households' recorded the highest private education expenditures.
○ The average monthly private education expenditures of 'Dual income households' 
recorded 459 thousand won per student, going up by 6.2% from 2022.
- The average monthly expenditures of 'Single income households by fathers' and 'Single income 
households by mothers' recorded 429 thousand won and 288 thousand won, respectively.
As for the average monthly private education expenditures, the share of students 
spending 700 thousand won or more stood at 22%, up 2.9%p from 2022.
○ As for the average monthly private education expenditures, the share of students spending 
less than 600 thousand won showed a year-on-year decrease. Whereas, the share of 
students spending 600 thousand won or more showed a year-on-year increase.
As for the private education of general subjects by purpose, 'Makeup for classes' 
occupied the highest share. As for the private education of artistic and physical 
skills by purpose, 'Hobbies, culture and development of talent' occupied the 
highest share.
○ As for the purposes of general subjects, 'Makeup for classes' occupied the highest share 
at 49.6%, which was followed by 'Study in advance' (24.0%) and 'Preparation for higher 
school level' (14.2%). Compared to 2022, 'Child care' went up by 0.7%p. Whereas, 
'Makeup for classes' and 'Anxiety' recorded a decrease.
○ As for the purposes of artistic and physical skills, 'Hobbies, culture and development 
of talent' occupied the highest share at 63.0%. Compared to 2022, 'Child care' went 
up by 0.9%p. Whereas, 'Hobbies, culture and development of talent' and 'Makeup for 
classes' recorded a decrease.
The after-school program participation rate stood at 41.0%. The percentage of 
students purchasing EBS textbooks stood at 16.1%. The language course 
participation rate stood at 0.5%.
○ Compared to 2022, the after-school program participation rate went up by 4.8%p. The 
percentage of students purchasing EBS textbooks went down by 0.3%p. The language 
course participation rate went up by 0.3%p.

The total private education expenditures of elementary, middle and high school 
students recorded 27.1 trillion won in 2023, rising by 4.5% from 2022.

recorded 27.1 trillion won in 2023, rising by 1.2 trillion won (4.5%) from 26 trillion won

recorded 7.2 trillion won in 2023, rising by 1.0% from 2022. The private education expenditures 
of high school students recorded 7.5 trillion won in 2023, rising by 8.2% from 2022.

persons, which fell by 70 thousand persons (-1.3%) from 2022.

2. Private education participation rate and weekly participation hours
The private education participation rate of elementary, middle and high school 
students stood at 78.5% in 2023, up 0.2%p from 2022. The weekly participation 
hours recorded 7.3 hours per student in 2023, which grew by 0.1 hour from 
2022.
○ The participation rate of elementary school students recorded the highest figure at 86.0%, 
which was followed by middle school students (75.4%) and high school students (66.4%).
- Compared to 2022, the participation rate of elementary and high school students went up by 
0.8%p and 0.5%p, respectively. The participation rate of middle school students went down by 
0.8%p.
○ The weekly participation hours of elementary school students recorded the highest figure 
of 7.5 hours, which was followed by middle school students (7.4 hours) and high school 
students (6.7 hours).
- Compared to 2022, the weekly participation hours of elementary and high school students went 
up by 0.1 hour and 0.1 hour, respectively. The weekly participation hours of middle school students 
went down by 0.1 hour.

4. Average monthly private education expenditures per student by subject
The average monthly private education expenditures on general subjects amounted 
to 326 thousand won per student in 2023, going up by 5.3% from 2022. The average 
monthly private education expenditures on general subjects amounted to 518 thousand 
won per participation student in 2023, rising by 5.7% from 2022.
○ As for the average monthly private education expenditures on general subjects, English 
recorded the highest figure of 128 thousand won per student, which was followed by 
mathematics (122 thousand won), Korean (38 thousand won) and 'Social studies and 
science' (19 thousand won).
- Compared to 2022, the average monthly private education expenditures on Korean (11.1%), 'Social 
studies and science' (8.2%), mathematics (5.6%) and English (3.8%) recorded an increase.
- As for the average monthly private education expenditures on general subjects by grade, 5th grade 
elementary school students spent the highest expenditures on English (131 thousand won). 3rd 
grade middle school students spent the highest expenditures on mathematics (174 thousand won). 
1st grade high school students spent the highest expenditures on mathematics (194 thousand won).
○ As for the average monthly private education expenditures on general subjects, English 
recorded the highest figure of 248 thousand won per participation student, which was 
followed by mathematics (233 thousand won), Korean (148 thousand won) and 'Social 
studies and science' (137 thousand won).
- Compared to 2022, the average monthly private education expenditures on Korean (8.2%), 'Social 
studies and science' (7.4%), mathematics (5.9%) and English (5.0%) recorded an increase.
- As for the average monthly private education expenditures on general subjects by grade, 5th grade 
elementary school participation students spent the highest expenditures on English (221 thousand 
won). 3rd grade middle school participation students spent the highest expenditures on mathematics 
(294 thousand won). 2nd and 3rd grade high school participation students spent the highest 
expenditures on mathematics (367 thousand won).
