## Reading against the paper's insurance claim (added at close-out, same run; no re-run)
The predeclared "outside-option decline does the work" branch is NOT met: the outside-option decline
LOWERS Q3-5 college entry by -0.0875 (2sd 0.0413), in the same direction as degree-premium compression
(-0.0991). The predeclared `contradicted` clause for the insurance reading therefore applies:
  - outside-option decline does NOT raise investment in massabm; it reduces it;
  - the only arms that raise Q3-5 investment are the human-capital/liquidity benchmarks (cost_down
    +0.120, borrow_up +0.104);
  - volatility up does raise Q1 entry (+0.025 vs baseline 0.023) while lowering Q3-5 (-0.036), a
    quintile-heterogeneous response consistent with an insurance motive at the bottom, not with a
    general "invest more as the outside option falls" claim.
So the investment response here is feasibility/liquidity-driven, not outside-option/insurance-driven.
Caveat: one-factor-at-a-time, 4 paired reps, simulated mechanism comparison, not a country estimate.
