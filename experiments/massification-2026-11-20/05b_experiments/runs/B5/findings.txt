Read (stylized model, NOT empirical hiring estimates):
  - a detailed domestic reader and a skill test both beat a coarse credential reader on hiring
    error at the declared baseline; the coarse reader pays for discarding within-tier ability.
  - the skill test is not free: it ignores the schooling component of productivity (omega>0),
    so its advantage over the detailed reader is bounded and it has near-zero certification
    demand by construction (declared limitation, not a finding).
  - screening costs and SES-linked networks convert reader quality into SES exclusion; the
    cost/network cells, not the reader choice alone, produce the largest SES hire gap.
  - cross-border recognition (rho 1.0 -> 0.3) raises foreign-reader error and top-tertile
    exclusion and lowers coverage of the genuinely best applicants.
