# U4 — WHAT IS PUBLISHABLE (after loop-3 stop rule + loop-4 identification round)

## The exact claim the model can support

**A negative/identification map plus one provider-economics result — not an incidence or massification
forecast.**

1. **Identification map (negative).** Stage-matched shadow incidence is **not reproducible** under a
   predeclared stop rule: loop-3 S1 failed 3/3 assessable countries (HKG gradient, KHM, PHL) and loop-4
   U1b failed the single-country rescue test (1998/99 HKG gradient: shape right — monotone, rank
   correlation 1.0 — but S1-3 +16.8 pp and S4-5 +12.1 pp outside ±15 pp, provider-mix ordering reversed,
   fee 5.19% vs 1.1–5.0%). **The claim "shadow education appears where the literature says" is withdrawn
   for all countries.** Recovery/forced-ID (U2) is **negative** (recovery 0.50 < 0.67; forced-ID 0.60 ≠ 0;
   100% without a placebo), so mechanism classification is not identified either.
2. **Provider economics (positive, one country).** The price/entry block is **usable for Korea**: with
   one participation calibration (78.5%), the model's per-participant annual fee/GDP-pc ratio is **13.5%
   vs the observed 13.8%** (per-student 10.6% vs 10.8%), both inside the predeclared bands. This converts
   loop-3 S3's "UNABLE" into a real single-country pass on the *price level*, while the demand block
   fails KOR by-level participation (official declines 86→75→66; model rises 72→79→88), has no hours
   object (7.3 h/wk UNABLE), and cannot produce the by-level spend gradient (uniform price) or the
   observed provider mix (model 83% commercial vs hagwon 55.5% of spend).
3. **Dynamic/country claims (negative).** Holdout U3: PHL held-out MAE 14.68 pp vs 5.70 naive → rejected;
   KHM passes the predeclared threshold only mechanically (flat path, in-sample RMSE 14.8 pp, s0 at the
   grid edge) → not a validated country claim. No country completion or massification claim is supported.
4. **Sample/simulation claims are conditional.** Everything above is stylized model output; the only
   positive number is model-vs-observed on KOR official 2023 fee moments (one country, one moment set).

## The one claim the model cannot support

It **cannot** support "shadow education appears where the literature says" (cross-country incidence), nor
"we can classify education-led vs demand-led vs complementary mechanisms from these moments" (U2
negative), nor any country-level massification trajectory (U3).

## Publishable object (recommended framing)

1. **The identification map**: a table of which claims are identified, which are UNABLE (target/data
   missing), and which are rejected at predeclared thresholds — with the stop-rule events recorded
   (loop-3 S1; loop-4 U1b, U2, U3-PHL). This is a contribution about what credential/shadow-education data
   can and cannot identify.
2. **Provider economics as a bounded module**: the fee/entry block calibrated and tested on the only
   country with verified fee moments (Korea 2023: 13.5% vs 13.8%), reported as a single-country check,
   explicitly not a cross-country validation; the teacher-tutor/commercial mix is a modelling object whose
   identification requires price data absent for PH/HK/KHM.
3. **A data-first program**: the binding constraint is data, not model structure — a single peer-reviewed
   HKG stage-gradient survey (n=630), one Korean official expenditure survey, and case-study KHM bounds do
   not identify a cross-country incidence model. Priorities: (i) verified fee/breadth moments for PH/KHM
   provider types; (ii) repeated within-country stage gradients; (iii) a pre-registered out-of-sample check
   with a hold-out wave; (iv) provider-entry/capacity data (teacher-tutor participation, fees, class size)
   to identify the supply side.
4. **The mechanism claim is not publishable yet**: U2 negative means the paper should not claim to
   distinguish education-led vs demand-led vs complementary from these moments without a placebo design or
   external instruments.

**Bottom line.** Publishable now = the negative/identification map + the single-country Korean
provider-economics check + the data-first program. Not publishable = cross-country shadow-incidence,
mechanism classification, or country massification forecasts.
