## D. Bottom line for the modelling program
1. **The analytic gate literature (Yoo, Kim, He) is fully in hand and is sufficient** to justify the fixed-bar vs rank-quota distinction and the defensive-investment result. These three are the formal spine.
2. **Kang (2026) is the only *estimated* positional-race model** available and the only source with a defensible decomposition of competition-driven tutoring. It should be the benchmark the ABM is compared against, not merely cited.
3. **Yang (2026) is the highest-risk unread source**: a published ranking + OLG + intergenerational model of tutoring regulation, calibrated on China. Trey's novelty claim depends on features absent from its abstract (informality floor, foreign readers, capture) — but the *equation form* attributed to Yang must be checked before submission.
4. **The state/empirical layer is the weakest verified part of the project.** Of ten or so load-bearing numeric claims about the Philippines, none was verifiable this session; the two most consequential (67.0% completion; ≈9 nurses per migrant) are `unresolved`.
