# ERROR LOG — model2 (`10_massification/model2/`)

## Run-level
- Final run: scaling estimation (2 samples × 11/7 pairs × up to 5 estimators), logistic fits, common-parameter
  tests, lead-lag, rivals (23 countries), and the simulated push–pull recovery (4 truths × 5 seeds).
  **0 failed cells** after two implementation fixes applied before results were recorded.

## Defects found and fixed before results recorded
1. **Lead-lag bootstrap index bug** (arrays of mismatched length when block-bootstrapping at negative lags)
   — crashed the first run; fixed by indexing both series with the same block index.
2. **Residual unpacking bug in the recovery test** — `ols()` returns (coef, se, residuals, r2); the first
   version summed squared *standard errors* instead of squared residuals, producing nonsense AIC
   (all truths → supply-led). Fixed in all five regressions; final recovery numbers reported.

## Interpretation issues / caveats
1. **Observed vs simulated is labelled**: Part 4 is SIMULATED; Parts 1–3 are estimation on observed data.
   No imputed panel (`gmr_annual_1980_2020_imputed.csv`) was used; no imputed cell treated as observation.
2. **`10_massification/data/` and `relations/` were EMPTY at run time** — fallback inputs declared in
   `PRE_DECLARATIONS_M2.md`; re-run required when these land.
3. **Scaling classification**: only three WDI30 relations meet the predeclared "scaling law" bar (GER
   secondary, GER tertiary, lower-sec completion vs income); UIS6 pairs are regularities due to small N;
   GER primary is unidentified (saturation); returns vs schooling is UNABLE (no earnings microdata).
4. **Common-parameter logistic test**: common-r model did not converge cleanly (AIC not finite); reported as
   `shared=False` from the comparison of available AIC values. This is a weak test at this sample.
5. **Lead-lag is NOT IDENTIFIABLE** at this sample (only 2 countries with usable differenced series) — the
   +1y median peak lag is not evidence; reported as non-identification, not as a lead.
6. **Rivals are observationally close**; the recovery test shows the push–pull verdict cannot be made
   (forced-ID = 1.00). This is the key negative result, not a failure of the run.
7. **Complementary truth recovery 0.00** — the candidate set's complementary rival is never selected when
   data are generated by the complementary world; recorded as a model-design limitation.
8. Closed shadow-education claims were not re-fit; shadow education is secondary in this phase.
