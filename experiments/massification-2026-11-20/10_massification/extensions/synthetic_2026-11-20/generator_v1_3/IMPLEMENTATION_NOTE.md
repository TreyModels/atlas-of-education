## 1. The one genuine spec gap: which v1.2 country carries which declared country type

The frozen predeclaration (`§2`, `§7`) needs three synthetic country types — `SYN_HKG_LIKE`, `SYN_PHL_LIKE`,
`SYN_KHM_LIKE` — with gate state `R` "computed from the run" (`§7`). **The frozen v1.2 generator contains no
HKG-like and no KHM-like country.** It has 30 synthetic countries: one declared `SYN00_PHL_LIKE`, two declared
twins, three declared cohort variants, and 24 drawn countries (`generator_v1_2/PARAMS.md` §9). There is no
on-disk HKG or KHM parameterisation, and fabricating one would be exactly the kind of undeclared country input
that `c4c` exists to prevent.

**Resolution (declared here, before any shadow output exists).** The shadow block is layered on the
**already-generated v1.2 gate state** of three designated v1.2 countries, selected by a rule that reads **only a
v1.2 state variable (2020 GDP per capita) and never a shadow quantity**:

| declared type | host v1.2 country | selection rule |
|---|---|---|
| `SYN_PHL_LIKE` | `SYN00_PHL_LIKE` | v1.2's own declared PHL-informed country (`PARAMS.md` §9); the only country whose trait block is anchored on an observed PHL path |
| `SYN_HKG_LIKE` | the v1.2 country with the **highest 2020 `gdppc`** | HKG is the high-income system in the declared set; the generator's richest synthetic economy is the declared analogue |
| `SYN_KHM_LIKE` | the v1.2 country with the **lowest 2020 `gdppc`** | KHM is the poorest of the three; the generator's poorest synthetic economy is the declared analogue |

The rule is implemented in code (`resolve_hosts()`), asserted to yield three distinct countries, and the resolved
names are written into `results/shadow_country_inputs_audit.csv` and `RUN_MANIFEST.json`. Ties would be broken by
lowest country index; none occur.

**What this does NOT do.** It does not alter any v1.2 parameter, cohort path, income path, sector mix or draw.
Each host country is generated by v1.2 exactly as delivered; the shadow block only *reads* its gate state. The
three declared `φ, s, σ, ν` bundles are overlaid on that state, as `§7` requires. `SYN_HKG_LIKE` is therefore
*the richest v1.2 synthetic economy evaluated under the HKG-like declared input bundle*, and is not Hong Kong
(and `SYN00_PHL_LIKE` is not the Philippines).

**Why this is not a rescue.** The rule is fixed before the checks are scored, depends only on an income variable
that is not an argument of the demand equation (`§4`, `SHADOW_MODULE.md` §4: `X, Tstate, Y, lit, sec` are
deliberately **not** inputs to demand), and cannot be varied without rewriting this note. The two hosts were not
selected on `D`, `R`, or any check outcome. The design phase *did* print the 2020 gate-state extents of the
candidate hosts while writing the gate-state extractor (see `TUNE_LOG.md` D1); that is disclosed, and no parameter
or threshold was changed as a result (the parameters are frozen; no v1.3 worker may change them).

## 2. Gate-state contract actually implemented (frozen `DRY_RUN_PLAN.md` §1.3)

`§1.3` is written in **v1 naming** (`seat[L]*Cann[L]`, `intake[L]`). v1.2's `seats` is a **count of adequate
places**, not a coverage ratio, and its admission constraint is the model's own rationing line
(`sim_v1_2.py:587`: `phys = min(phys_cap*seats, overage_cap*C)`). The mapping is therefore:

| gate | candidates | capacity | advances | `κ` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| g0 (control) | primary cohort `C[0,t]` | `seats[0,t]` | `enrol[0,t]` (official-age admissions) | 1.0 (no credential rank at a non-gate) |
| g1 | `grads[0,t]` | `phys[1,t] = min(phys_cap·seats[1], overage_cap·C[1,t])` | `enrol[1,t]` | `rankf[0,t]` |
| g2 | `grads[1,t]` | `phys[2,t]` | `enrol[2,t]` | `rankf[1,t]` |
| g3 | `grads[2,t]` | `phys[3,t]` | `enrol[3,t]` | `rankf[2,t]` |
| g4 | `Σ_{r=1..3}(queue_r + hires_r)` | `Σ_{r=1..3} vac_r` | `Σ_{r=1..3} hires_r` | `hires`-weighted mean of `rankf[1..3]` |

`Q = candidates/capacity` clipped `[0,10]`; `π⁰ = advances/candidates` clipped `[0.01,0.999]`;
`κ` clipped `[1.0,5.0]`; `R = (1+Q)^0.40 · (1/π⁰)^0.40 · κ^0.20` clipped `[1.0,20.0]`. The three components are
written out separately (`shadow_gates_v1_3.csv`) so a reader can see which drives `R`. The demand equation never
sees `X, Y, Tstate, lit, sec` (`§4`, `SHADOW_MODULE.md` §4).

`hires_r` is **not stored** by v1.2 (`§3` lists it as read-only state, but the frozen panel omits it). v1.3 stores
the *already-computed* `hires` array; this is a diagnostic store only and changes no arithmetic. `c6` proves that:
with the block off, the panel is byte-identical to v1.2.

**`g2` wording.** `§2`'s gate table describes `g2`'s decisive transition as "(ter eligibility / grads₂)", which
reads as tertiary entry and conflicts with its own gate name (`GATE-US`), with `§4.2` (`ΔS_g2 = 3` years, the
upper-secondary credential), and with `DRY_RUN_PLAN.md` §1.3's explicit contract (`grads[1]`, `seat[2]`,
`intake[2]`). The concrete contract in `§1.3` is followed: **g2 = entry into upper secondary, candidates =
lower-secondary graduates**. No other reading is consistent with the gate names, `ΔS`, or `DRY_RUN_PLAN` §1.3.

## 3. Other declared implementation choices (none is a parameter)

1. **`s_{c,g} = s_c` for every gate.** `PARAMETERS.csv` declares one fee share per country (C04–C06), not a
   gate-specific fee. The `g` subscript in `§4.4` is therefore carried as constant in `g`.
2. **`S` is a share of mean monthly household income, not money.** No household-income series exists on disk for
   the synthetic countries, so `S_{c,g,t} = D_{c,g,t} · s_c` in declared fee units = *shares of mean monthly
   household income*. No currency conversion is invented.
3. **`V_{g4}` from the model's own wage block.** `V_{g4} = φ_c · G_c · H` with
   `G_c = ln(w_for[rung2]/w_inf)` at the same year (`PARAMETERS.csv` B21), clipped at `V ≥ 0` for numerical
   safety; whether the clip binds is reported (it does not).
4. **Feedback hook (`§5`, `DRY_RUN_PLAN.md` §1.2).** The realised advance rate `Π` is substituted into the
   **next** period's pool for the level above: `newpool[L] = grads[L-1,t] · (Π_{gL,t}/π⁰_{gL,t}) +
   pool_retain·pools[L]`, one-year lag, `L = 1,2,3` (`g1,g2,g3`). `g4` is a labour-market gate: `Π_{g4}` is
   computed and reported but has **no hook** into v1.2's matching (the frozen block may only modify the realised
   advance rate on the school pipeline; `§5`). For `t = 0` the ratio is 1.
5. **The feedback is bounded by construction**: `Π = (1−D)π⁰ + Dπ¹`, so `|Π − π⁰| = D·δ_g(1−π⁰) ≤ δ_g(1−π⁰)`.
   The ratio is guarded to `[1, 1+δ_g(1−π⁰)/π⁰]` and its realised value is written out.
6. **Comparison year `t* = 2020`** for `c1(ii)`, `c2(ii)`, `c4a` and `c4b` (`§8` fixes `t* = 2020` for `c4a`;
   the same year is used wherever a single year is needed, and the full 1970–2025 path is reported beside it).
7. **Saturation rule for `c1(i)/c2(i)`.** `DRY_RUN_PLAN.md` §7.2 pre-declares that the pp-based checks are
   informative only where `D ∈ [0.05, 0.90]`, and that a saturated cell's `c1/c2` component is `NOT-SCORED`
   rather than counted as a pass. A cell with `D ∉ [0.05, 0.90]` is therefore `NOT-SCORED` (reported, with the
   count) and is excluded from the sign verdict; `c1(i)`'s PASS requires the sign to hold in **every scored cell**.
   The derivative is computed **numerically** (central difference, `h = 1e-4` in `ln R` / `ln(1+V)`) and the
   analytic value `b_Z·Σω p(1−p)` is reported beside it as a diagnostic.
8. **`c5` "tutored head-count ≤ cohort at gate"** is implemented as
   `D_g · candidates_g ≤ C_{L(g),t} · (1+1e-12)` for `g1..g3` (destination-level cohort) and
   `D_g4 · candidates_g4 ≤ candidates_g4` (no cohort ceiling exists at the labour gate). The maximum ratio is
   reported.
9. **`c6` ablation artifact.** The ablation run writes `_ablation/synthetic_panel_v1_3_shadow_off.csv` with
   **exactly** v1.2's 67 columns, row order and `%.10g` formatting, and `c6` compares its sha256 to the frozen
   `generator_v1_2/results/synthetic_panel_v1_2.csv`. The scored panel additionally carries shadow columns.
10. **Determinism.** One `PCG64` stream, seed `20261120`, inherited unchanged from v1.2; all draws happen in
    v1.2's original order (the shadow block draws nothing). Floats `%.10g`.
