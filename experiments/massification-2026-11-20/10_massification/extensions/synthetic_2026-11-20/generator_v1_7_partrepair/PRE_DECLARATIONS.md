## 4. The repair, stated exactly (one substitution, no new parameter)

The object under test is the **frozen v1.6 code**, copied byte-for-byte into
`code/frozen/sim_v1_6_repair.py` (sha256 `9c0e7998…`, byte-identical to
`generator_v1_6_repair/code/sim_v1_6_repair.py`). The frozen v1.3 block is copied
byte-for-byte into `code/frozen/sim_v1_3.py` (sha256 `70ae8f0e…`). **Neither copy is edited.**
The repair replaces exactly one module-level function lookup in the imported frozen module
(`tier_cells`), never the frozen file.

**What is substituted (the whole of it).** In the frozen v1.3 block, who is school-going is
decided by the declared four-state block `state_shares(cp, level, y_shock)` — the multinomial
logit over `{school, both, work, neither}` with its declared primitives `NEED = 0.85`,
`UTIL_SCALE = 0.30`, `GIRL_SHARE = 0.50`, `TASTE = 0.0`, `ABILITY = 0.0`, `OUT_YEARS = 0.0`,
`AGE_W = (0.25, 0.70, 1.00)`, the declared `cost_base = (0.05, 0.14, 0.22)` and the declared
`cp` fields `y_med, w_child, farm, cost, v0, decay, compat, neither, girl_care`. Its
school-going share is `ds13 = Pr[school] + Pr[both]`; its out-of-school split is
`(Pr[work], Pr[neither])`. The repaired arm **calls that frozen function** and uses its two
outputs; **nothing is re-implemented and no number is introduced**.

**Operative rule (deterministic, no RNG), per level `L < 3` and year `t`:**
1. `Pr = sim_v1_3.state_shares(cp, L, y_shock)`; `ds13 = Pr[0] + Pr[1]`; `(pw, pn) = (Pr[2], Pr[3])`.
2. Per cell `k` (5 income × 3 ability): `w_school[k] = w[k] · ds13`, `w[k]` = the declared
   `S01` joint weights (sum 1). The block has no income/ability dimension, so the mass is
   uniform across cells **by construction** — the block's own structure, not a smoothing
   choice.
3. The tier block's own payoff comparison splits that mass: `first = H` iff `U_H[k] > U_O[k]`,
   else `O` (tie → `O`, the frozen convention); the frozen `U_H, U_O` formulas and the frozen
   menu values are used unchanged.
4. The frozen `tier_allocate` is called with `w = w_school`, `first ∈ {H, O}`, `U_O`, `u_out`
   unchanged, and the frozen `K_H, K_O`; its result is used unchanged **except** the
   out-of-school composition `(mW, mB, mN)`, overwritten with `(pw, pn, 0)` — a **reporting**
   object only (declared, no effect on demand, admission, capacity or any verdict object).
5. `demand_official = A_H + A_O_first` as in v1.6 (the D-2 unduplicated applicant object).

**What is NOT substituted (the structural part).** The tier menu — `T01 h_share`, `T05
q_O = 0.55`, `T06 cost_H_mult = 1.60`, `T07 cost_O_mult = 0.03`, `T12 a_gate = 0.40`, `T13
risk_pen = 0.20`, `T14 H_h = 20.0`, `S01–S08` — and the frozen `tier_allocate` ranking, the
**one-place-per-pupil tier capacity** `K_H + K_O = seats`, the survival law, the capacity
build-out rule and the whole economy block are **held exactly as built**. The O-versus-outside
refusal margin `acc: U_O > u_out` is kept, because that margin is where the menu's declared
quality discount acts (R4a).
