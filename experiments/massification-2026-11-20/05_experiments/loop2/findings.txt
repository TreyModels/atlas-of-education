VERDICT:
  The freeze tests show what moves what. If attainment still rises with the wage/job feedback frozen,
  AND formal jobs still rise with schooling feedback frozen, then neither is generated by the other:
  the two-way path is largely a SCHEDULE in this structure (consistent with C2 floorworld).
  Identifiability: calibration moves the fitted point INSIDE a family; C1 showed surplus and
  competitive CES are not distinguishable on aggregate moments. Calibration therefore cannot make
  them identifiable. What would: gate-specific shadow take-up (data blocked), formal-vs-informal
  transition rates by credential, density near admission cutoffs, and panel shock responses.
  Forced-identification rate: inherited from C1 = 2/2 (outside truth absorbed by the surplus family);
  this loop adds no separating moment, so the rate cannot improve until the shadow/transition
  moments land.
