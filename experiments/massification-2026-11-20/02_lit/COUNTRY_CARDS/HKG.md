**Status of the 85% number.** This is the claim the June draft carries ("Hong Kong's 85 percent senior secondary tutoring rate is not a cultural preference..."). Its **provenance is now verified**: it is Bright's-provenance Bray & Lykins (2012) p. X, and the Jukupedia post reproduces the book's own sentence. It is **not** a first-hand reading of the book's table, and the 2014 EER paper that would give grade/provider detail could not be retrieved. **Confidence: medium-high on provenance, medium on the exact denominator ("senior secondary") and year.**

## 2. Gates
- **HKDSE** (first cohort 2012) replaced HKCEE (O-level) + HKALE (A-level) - a single high-stakes norm-referenced terminal exam with fine-grained levels.
- **JUPAS** centralised university admission: a single transaction allocating university + programme + future position (the October paper's "near-lossless positional resolution" example).
- **Secondary School Places Allocation (SSPA)** at primary->secondary transition - the earlier sorting gate.
- Pass rates / Level-4+ shares by year: **NOT FOUND** this session (HKEAA reports not retrieved).

- Senior-secondary participation, tertiary entrance, dropout: **NOT FOUND** this session.

## 4. Economy context
- High-income city-state, credential-intensive service economy, emigration pressures; **no verified numbers imported this session**.

## 5. Policy timeline
- 2009 new senior secondary curriculum; **2012 first HKDSE cohort** (replacing HKCEE/HKALE); 2017+ National Security Education replacing Liberal Studies (both manuscripts raise this as a content-robustness test case); **dates not independently verified this session**.

## 6. Disagreements / unverifiable
- **The single biggest unknown is the denominator.** "Senior secondary" in Bray & Lykins is not the same population as "all secondary students" or "Secondary 4-7"; a 2014 EER paper exists precisely to disaggregate this. Until read, the model should treat 85% as an *upper-bound, upper-secondary-only* target, not a system-wide rate.
- No income-quintile or provider split was retrievable. HKG therefore supplies a **level** target (85%) and **no structure** target.
