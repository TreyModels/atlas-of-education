## 2. Gates
- **Zhongkao** (junior-secondary leaving exam): streams into academic vs vocational upper secondary - the ~50% vocational-streaming rule is the key gate; **exact ratio and year NOT VERIFIED**.
- **Gaokao**: provincial quota-based university admission with cutoffs varying by province and hukou registration - the archetypal **rank-quota** gate in Trey's typology (and the gate Yoo's quota model and He's mean-plus-kσ model both formalise).
- **Hukou** as a non-academic eligibility gate on schooling and university access at the destination.

## 6. Disagreements / unverifiable
- **The central empirical question for the project** - did Double Reduction reduce total household investment at the gaokao gate, or reroute it to informal/residential channels? - is **exactly the question the retrieved literature could not answer**, because the survey evidence sits behind the CNKI and IAFOR walls. The October paper asserts rerouting ("the gate survived; the intensity survived"); that is currently an **unverified claim** and should be carried as a research proposition, not a finding.
- Everything quantitative about CHN in this card is absent by necessity, not by choice. Mark CHN "data-thin" in all downstream reports.
