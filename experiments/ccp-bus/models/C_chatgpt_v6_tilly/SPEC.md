# V6 Tilly extension: frozen development specification

18 November 2026. Written before experiment execution; no public preregistration is claimed.

Purpose: test whether initially clean, high-integrity central appointees become involved in misconduct through resource-dependent relationships under sustained enforcement. This is an explanatory computational experiment with unestimated coefficients.

All regimes appoint one synthetic outsider to each organizational head seat at the start of 2013. These people have no modeled pre-appointment misconduct, high integrity, high competence and high loyalty. Cohort membership is immutable person identity. The replacement intervention is an experimental construction and does not identify historical people selected by Xi.

New mechanisms:

- Resource-dependent requests and organizational closure in supplier access. Legitimate assistance and insider membership do not automatically constitute corruption.
- Explicit person-to-person favor obligations. Assistance creates obligations; lawful services can discharge them. Illicit repayment requires a diverted resource pool and participation by both counterparties. Departures extinguish the relevant obligations with recorded write-offs.
- Public allocation accounting and redistribution of diverted resources. Public delivery plus diversion equals allocated budget; illicit transfers redistribute diversion without creating funds.
- Organizational adaptation to demand and emulation of more successful observable organizational performance. Emulation sees public output and enforcement events, not latent misconduct.
- Protection supplied following actual corrupt repayment, influencing future reporting/detection. Promotion support can reward delivered favors, with a merit-only comparison.
- Direct appointment-cohort event histories: exposure, first misconduct, true/false filing, sanctions, retirement and employment removal. Delayed sanctions can follow retirement. No successor inherits cohort membership or accusations.

Comparisons include campaign vs baseline, access reform, obligation/protection/emulation/adaptation ablations, equal resource allocation across ranks, stronger oversight, low demand, organizational relationship reset, merit-only selection and a new-mechanisms-off regime.

Evaluation separates misconduct incidence, extraction amounts, legitimate dependencies, latent ties and observed enforcement. Rank gradients are conditional implications of resource assignment; equal-resource controls test this assumption. A removal is not itself treated as evidence of true misconduct. Zero risk sets produce undefined conditional rates, with denominators saved.

The fixed design will be saved before parallel execution. Parameter variation, seed MCSE, initialization checks and source/data hashes must accompany results. The existing annual official and procurement evidence is retained, with no empirical likelihood or CCP prevalence estimate asserted.

## Implementation reconciliation

This note was added while the executed batch was finishing. The saved manifest defines the realized comparisons. The added `no_replacement` arm supplies no outsider cohort and otherwise shares the campaign policy; `zero_attraction` changes only that coefficient. The initially described merit-only comparison was implemented more narrowly as `payment_blind`: it removes the new payment-support term while retaining the existing competence, loyalty and patronage terms. This clarification changes no executed behavior.
