(11 country-waves; full table in `contrasts_final.csv`.) Monotone positive wealth gradient everywhere, widest in

### 3.2 Residence gap (E2, urban − rural, 14–17)
Positive in all 19 waves (+0.0148 PH 2017 to +0.263 ID 1994); largest gaps in the older ID/VN waves, smallest in
PH 2017/2022. This is a descriptive composition contrast, not a school-quality effect.

### 3.3 Sex gap (E3, male − female, 14–17)
Cambodia **reverses**: +0.235 (2000) → +0.112 (2005) → +0.083 (2010) → +0.019 (2014) → **−0.055 (2021-22)**.
Philippines shows a female advantage throughout (−0.044 → −0.071 → −0.035); ID/VN are small and mixed
(+0.018 to +0.070 in older ID/VN waves; −0.024 ID 2017). Reversal is a descriptive cross-section pattern.

### 3.4 Completed fertility by schooling (E4, women 40–49, 12+ minus 0–5 years)
KH 2005 −3.24 (0.27); KH 2010 −2.25 (0.15); KH 2014 −1.69 (0.14); KH 2021-22 −0.81 (0.13); **PH 2022 −2.43
(0.15)**. KH 2000 "12+" suppressed (n=25). The KH narrowing is a completed-cohort association; selection/survival
and survey eligibility apply, and education is measured at the survey — **not a schooling effect**.

### 3.5 Early birth and early union by schooling (E5, E7; women 25–34)
E5 (birth <20): −0.18 (KH 2005) to −0.26 (KH 2014/2021-22), −0.41 (PH 2022). E7 (union <20): −0.26 to −0.39
(KH), −0.55 (PH 2022). **No narrowing of the E7 gap**: KH 2000 −0.39 → 2021-22 −0.39. Schooling may postdate
early birth/union, so these are associations only.

### 3.6 Current school participation (E6, women 20–24, urban − rural)
Positive and large in ID/VN waves (+0.10 to +0.15 in most; +0.16 VN 2005ais); PH 1993/1998/2017 small
(+0.024 to +0.046), PH 2022 negative (−0.030, BH q=0.16). E6 is a **person-record definition** (D-01 approved
deviation from the README woman-recode spec; no DHS IR current-attendance item exists).

### 3.7 Household size by schooling (E8, women 40–49, 12+ minus 0–5)
**KH: null** (all BH q ≥ 0.39; −0.32 to +0.10). **PH 2022: −0.97 (0.13)**, the largest contrast in the set.
This is current household size — **not kin support** — and the PH result is a genuine negative finding against
using household size as a kin-support proxy in models.

## 9. Limitations (binding disclosures)

Descriptive within-wave domain means only — **no causal claim**; schooling may postdate early birth/union; wealth
quintiles are relative asset indices, not income; household size is current household composition, not kin
support; repeated cross-sections are not a panel and no persons are linked; education is measured at the survey;
KH 2021-22 is COVID-distorted (flagged); normal-approximation CIs and Holm/BH multiplicity are reported, with
design df; few-country design (2–4 countries for E4–E8) limits cross-country inference; 3 cells suppressed, 1
cell insufficient (KH 2000 E4 12+ n=25); benchmark coverage is partial (KH extraction only).
