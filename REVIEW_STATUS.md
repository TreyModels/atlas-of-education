## Problems found beyond the review

1. **Credential floor: hard liquidity threshold.** It makes a completion cliff by wealth, so no draw fits any quintile profile. A graded constraint fits, and the relational verdict disappears.
2. **Credential floor v1: two code errors.** It was not reproducible (seeded with Python's per-process `hash()`). Its `exit_low` was read in 2035, and the DHS bundle compared that value with 2021 data.
3. **Credential floor: projections are fragile.** The 2035 completion response changes sign with the form of the constraint.
4. **Angkor monsoon: extrapolation.** The reconstructed PDSI reaches −7.2 (1403), far outside the modern instrumental range, so any modern yield response is an extrapolation (`inputs/lit/rice_drought_response.md`).
5. **Out of Africa: front-speed prior.** It is wider than the literature (about 1–2 km/yr published, against a 0.86–5.08 km/yr prior), and the short chronology leans on the fast tail.
6. **Child labour: cost index base.** `cst()` charges unit costs to every child in a region, not only beneficiaries, so the index is not a programme cost even on its own assumptions.
7. **Youth: stale site data.** The site's `atlas_data.js` was not regenerated from the refits (rise 4.99 vs 5.06). The "+22.8 pp no-waiting" figure in `docs/engine/facts_register.md` does not reproduce (+12.2 now).
8. **Youth: insider premium.** The one twin study found (Li et al. 2007) shows a party-membership pay premium of about 0 with twin fixed effects. Open microdata with hukou, party and parental sector exist (CFPS, CCSS), so the insider/outsider claims are testable but untested (`inputs/lit/china_insider_outsider_data.md`).
9. **Shadow education: shared code.** The three explanations share one `model.py`, and only 6 of about 215 lines differ, so their agreement on policy is not independent evidence.
10. **Angkor relocation: water floor hid collapse.** Without the 0.1 floor, water at a centre with no local repair falls to about 0 (median 0.002). Old runs sat on the floor in at least half of cases. With local repair the median is about 0.76. The relocation page now states this.
