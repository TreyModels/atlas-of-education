Shadow education lab · Cambodia

EDUCATION & WORK · NEW · EXPLORATORY

Why do Cambodian pupils buy tutoring from their own teachers? An agent-based model of 72 teachers and 1,260 pupils tests three rival explanations: a thin timetable, teachers withholding content, and favour. All three fit Bray and colleagues' seven published patterns about equally, so the data cannot separate them, but they agree on policy. A ban moves demand elsewhere and lowers curriculum coverage; more class time is the only tested policy that raises it. The page also includes the DHS 2021–22 test that rejects the relational credential floor for Cambodian school leavers. English and Simplified Chinese.

Fertility collapse lab · Six Asian places

POPULATION & SCHOOL SYSTEMS · NEW · EXPLORATORY

Why fertility fell below 1 in Korea and toward 1 across East Asia. Development (child survival) explains the fall to about 1.4, held out one place at a time. Mechanism simulations add the credential race and a marriage-market squeeze, and a tipping model shows when a temporary shock does permanent damage. All six places fell below their development path after 2015. Several earlier claims were ruled out, including a 15–20 year lag, an age-29 ratchet and country-to-country spread. English and Simplified Chinese.

Elite relocation · Separate two-centre experiment

9,216 histories compare elite movement, worker movement and blocked movement. Outside opportunities and moving hurdles alter departure. Farming can continue, and water condition can improve or worsen depending on who leaves. Utilities and movement rates remain explicit assumptions.

Published economic foundation and separate Petri lab

Klassen, Ortman, Lobo and Evans provide the provisioning and spatial-equilibrium relationships. The browser explorer normalizes their published coefficients. A separate Atlas dynamic extension adds food stores, maintenance, growth, migration and declared droughts. Across 3,072 matched runs, its complete economy recovers; the coordination knockout can retain a persistent decline. These are exploratory model results, with no historical fit claim.

LIEBERMAN & BUCKLEY · UNDER ACTIVE EXAMINATION

Angkor · Seasonal campaign lab

UNDER ACTIVE EXAMINATION · EXPERIMENTAL V0.2

The Atlas experiment links campaign distance and time away to harvest labour, captive transfers, and provisioning. Timber, stone, fish and digging use the same seasonal accounts. Penny and colleagues’ published hydraulic-network model is a comparison for the water question. Its network equations are separate from this campaign engine.

We reran 18 modeled years for each of 32 matched seeds in six scenarios. The table reports differences inside the simulator.
