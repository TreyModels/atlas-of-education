ABOUT THE EVIDENCE

Models make ideas
testable.

These experiments use simplified rules and a small number of random seeds. They ask what can happen under stated assumptions—not what caused massification in the real world.

Parent peer pressure is inserted into the model. Exclusion and organizational inertia are imposed interventions. Removing an actor also changes other actors’ incentives. Some differences vary substantially by seed.
