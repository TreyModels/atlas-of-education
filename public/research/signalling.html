ANGKOR · UNDER ACTIVE EXAMINATION · EXPERIMENTAL

Would you pay tribute to the successor?

Two worlds inherit the same monument and elephants. In one, the tributary judges readiness from the display. In the other it can see how much defence the court can actually provision. The court receives tribute the year after a pledge. Each pair faces identical harvest shocks and frontier challenges.

What the model shows

Six scenarios, 32 paired seeds per scenario, 12 years per run. False reassurance means pledging despite the court’s inability to meet that year’s challenge. Defence and tribute counts compare the display world with its informed counterpart.

The display can keep tribute coming in despite failed defence. That tribute can then improve real capacity. Even without a succession loss the default signal is optimistic, and reducing trust sharply changes the result. Treat the magnitudes as consequences of the belief rule. Elephant maintenance reflects current revenue in the code, which helps explain why the elephant-only signal is less optimistic.

Try it

Set stone weight to zero to let the tributary judge by maintained elephants. Then put it back and lower trust in the display. Watch what happens to tribute and actual capacity. Finally, spend 45% on construction and compare pledged years with defended years. The model has finite stores, and building, keeping elephants and responding to a challenge all draw on the same grain and labour accounts.

Calculations run locally in your browser. The seasonal Campaign Lab and this experiment have separate budgets. What we borrowed and what we added

Costly signalling is a published explanation for monumental architecture. The Atlas adds a matched simulation of whether inherited stone and current elephant upkeep lead a tributary to different decisions, and whether tribute changes the outcome. The Angkor Wat historical gallery supplies evidence of elephants in royal display. Sandstone quarry research points to canal transport. This model does not reconstruct monument costs, military numbers or actual tributary beliefs. Its units are grain-equivalent and standardized labour, and its rules are illustrative.
