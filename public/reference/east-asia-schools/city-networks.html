What has been announced

PUBLISHED GUANGZHOU PLANS

Guangzhou is adding places

Guangzhou’s 2026 work plan calls for 50,000 additional public basic-education places, including 15,000 general senior-high places, and at least 10,000 additional general senior-high admissions. These are stated capacity and intake plans. They do not specify a net count of school operations or teachers, so the model shows them alongside the scenarios.

A 2025 article by Guangzhou’s education chief inspector projected peak enrollment in 2026 for primary, 2031 for junior secondary, and 2034 for senior high. The model’s default peak years agree. The article gives a timing benchmark. Enrollment and capacity after the peaks depend on the scenario assumptions.

The model uses Guangzhou’s 2020–2025 education bulletins. The primary-to-junior transition follows local cohorts. The citywide series cannot identify individual schools, staffing departures, or the geography of new public capacity. School counts and full-time teacher requirements are model sensitivities.
