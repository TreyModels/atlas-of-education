## 5. Hidden costs / no-collection policy
- Bray (1996), *Counting the Full Cost* (World Bank/UNICEF; nine East Asian countries including the Philippines), original checked 2026-11-22: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/267121468771877173/pdf/Counting-the-full-cost-parental-and-community-financing-of-education-in-East-Asia.pdf . The book treats education as a human right and finds fee-free primary schooling difficult to sustain on equity grounds when governments want to attract poor pupils. It does **not** advocate blanket fee-free provision at every level: it reports arguments for tertiary cost recovery with protection for poor students, and discusses budget and equity dilemmas at all levels. Its central empirical claim is that official government budgets omit substantial parental/community cash, labour, materials and opportunity costs, often regressively. Do not turn this into the claim that household payments prove demand independently of the state.
- DepEd Order 41 s.2012 (24 May 2012): no fees from K-Grade 4 in SY2012-13; Grade 5-HS no fees Jun-Jul, then voluntary contributions only; teachers may not collect or handle PTA funds; non-payment cannot block admission, promotion or clearance. https://deped.gov.ph/?p=8273 (Rel. high).
- DepEd Memorandum 41 (signed 2 Aug 2024): collection of contributions "in any form" prohibited, citing RA 4206 (one month jail or fine). https://philstar.com/headlines/2024/08/05/2375531/deped-reminds-schools-no-collection-policy (Rel. medium, news).
- No enforcement statistics found.
