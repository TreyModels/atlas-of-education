# Philippines: government demand for education and student flows (compiled 2026-11-22)

Method: World Bank API fetched one URL per indicator through WebFetch (values copied as returned; the fetch tool uses a small model to list pairs, so spot-check before heavy use). All other facts come from the cited URLs via search/fetch. Machine-readable copy: government_and_flows.json. Items not found are listed at the end; nothing was estimated.

## A1. World Bank series (full year lists are in the JSON)
URL pattern: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/PHL/indicator/CODE?format=json&per_page=100 (reliability: high for the source, moderate for transcription).
- SE.XPD.TOTL.GD.ZS (govt education spend, % GDP): 1980 1.51; 1995 2.66; 1998 3.70 (peak of 1990s); 2005 2.33 (trough); 2009 2.54; 2014 2.66; 2015 2.83; 2016 3.30; 2017 3.96; 2019 3.41; 2022 3.58; 2025 3.97. Gaps 2010-2013 and 1999, 1987-1994.
- SE.XPD.TOTL.GB.ZS (% of govt expenditure): 1995 15.2; 2005 12.4; 2009 13.2; 2015 15.1; 2017 19.6; 2019 18.2; 2025 15.2. Values for 1982, 1983, 1986 are 0 in the API: missing-data artefacts, ignore.
- SE.XPD.PRIM/SECO/TERT.PC.ZS (share of education spend by level, %): primary 12.5 (1997) -> 9.1 (2008); secondary 10.7 (1997) -> 9.2 (2008); tertiary 18.5 (1997) -> 9.7 (2008), 10.6 (2009). These are shares of an expenditure, not of the total, and look low (WB definition quirk); treat as indicative. No data after 2008/09.
- SE.COM.DURS (compulsory years): 10 for 1998-2011; 11 for 2012-2017; 13 for 2018-2025. Matches RA 10157 (2012) adding kindergarten and RA 10533 adding SHS, but the 2018 step lags the law (Grade 11 began 2016).
- SE.PRM.TCHR: 243,833 (1971); 314,838 (1990); 359,798 (2000); 423,035 (2010); 470,194 (2015); 482,743 (2017); 527,504 (2024).
- SE.SEC.TCHR: 51,979 (1971); 118,805 (1990); 148,033 (2001); 194,373 (2009); 267,515 (2014); 314,482 (2016); 377,174 (2017); 421,824 (2018); 495,843 (2024). The jump 2014-2018 is SHS hiring.

## A2. Targets in national plans (level, value, target year, plan, URL)
- PDP 2017-2022 Ch.10 (https://depdev.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/10-Chapter-10-Accelerating-Human-Capital-Development-1.14.2019.pdf), target year 2022: net enrolment kindergarten 74.65 (2015) -> 95; elementary 91.05 -> 95; junior high 68.15 -> 75.44; senior high TBD. Completion elementary 83.43 -> 90; junior high 73.97 -> 78.48; senior high TBD. Mean years of schooling 8.9 (2014) -> 11.3. Higher-ed scholarship/grant share 5% -> 10%; STEM-type graduate share 26% -> 40%; NEET 22.7% -> 15-18%; PESFA 25,000/yr 2019-22. No explicit tertiary participation target found. Reliability: moderate (PDF table read by summariser; two unlabeled rows dropped).
- Philippine EFA 2015 National Plan of Action (SDC-CL Resolution No. 2 s.2006, approved 1 Feb 2006) https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/Philippine_Education_For_All_EFA_2015_National_Plan_of_Action.pdf : targets are universal coverage/completion by 2015 with no numeric interim values. Baselines: SY2002-03 participation ages 6-11 = 90.32%; per 1,000 Grade 1 entrants 312 leave before Grade 6 and 439 graduate in 6 years; secondary per 1,000: 389 leave, 248 graduate in 4 years.
- K-12 rationale (Senate EPO policy brief PB 2011-02, https://web.senate.gov.ph/publications/PB%202011-02%20-%20K%20to%2012%20The%20Key%20to%20Quality.pdf): target 12 years of basic education; Philippines the only Asian country with a 10-year cycle; Washington Accord (1989) and Bologna (1999) cited; graduates under 18 cannot legally work. Also commitment to 100% net enrolment by 2015 (judged unlikely). Baselines: net elementary participation 90.1% (2002) -> 88.1% (2010); 2007 net participation elementary/secondary/tertiary 89.0/65.1/21.6 (poor 85.9/51.4/8.7); SY2011-12 shortages 152,569 classrooms, 103,599 teachers; DepEd 12.6% of 2011 budget = 2.7% of GDP. Advocacy document; costs preliminary.
- QBEDP 2025-2035 (https://planipolis.iiep.unesco.org/sites/default/files/ressources/Philippines_Quality-Basic-Education-Development-Plan-QBEDP-2025-to-2035-29-July-2025.pdf): NER baseline SY2023-24 / 2028 / 2031 / 2034 = kinder 78/96/97/98; elementary 86/89/92/97; JHS 78/81/83/84; SHS 67/69/71/71. Completion elementary 92/96/98/100; JHS 82/88/94/100; SHS 73/84/92/100. Classroom PPP cumulative 13,391 -> 35,391 (2028).
- PDP 2023-2028 education matrix: tables not extractable (headers only). BESRA, CHED roadmap, PDP 1978-2016: not retrieved.

## A3. Laws, programmes, credential rules
- CA 586 (Education Act of 1940), approved 6 Aug 1940: revised public elementary system and financing (https://ldr.senate.gov.ph/subject/education-act-of-1940). Compulsory ages not in extract.
- RA 10157 Kindergarten Education Act, 20 Jan 2012: kindergarten "mandatory and compulsory for entrance to Grade 1" (https://lpr.adb.org/resource/kindergarten-education-act-republic-act-no-10157-philippines ; date from https://lawyerly.ph/laws/view/l9270).
- RA 10533 Enhanced Basic Education Act, approved 15 May 2013 (https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/55631): at least 1 year kindergarten, 6 elementary, 4 JHS + 2 SHS; elementary and secondary called stages of "compulsory basic education"; policy of "globally competitive" education "at par with international standards".
- RA 10931 free tuition in SUCs/LUCs, signed 3 Aug 2017 (https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2017/08/04/1725170/headlines). CHED chair: enrolment 2.9M (2017) to 4.1M (2022), shift from private to public (https://www.abs-cbn.com/news/05/18/23/ched-reports-higher-enrollment-graduation-drop-out-rates-unchanged). FHE beneficiaries AY2018-19: 1,075,024 SUCs + 109,833 LUCs (PIDS seminar slide 33).
- CSC Resolution 2500229 (March 2025, announced 8 May 2025): SHS graduates eligible for first-level government jobs that previously required 2 years of college (https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2025/05/09/2441826/k-12-graduates-now-eligible-first-level-government-jobs). Shows civil service qualification standards tie jobs to credentials. Earlier CSC rules not retrieved.
- 4Ps households: 6,000 (2007 pilot); 340,391 (2008); 3,121,530 (Dec 2012); about 4.4M (2014); 3,949,855 regular CCT / 4,236,739 incl. MCCT (2018); 4,250,272 active, 5,066,892 registered cumulative (Dec 2019). Child age coverage 0-14 (2013) widened to 0-18 (2014/15). URLs: WB Pantawid 2017 assessment https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/457071570046480804/pdf/Pantawid-Pamilya-2017-Assessment-An-Update-of-the-Philippine-Conditional-Cash-Transfer-s-Implementation-Performance.pdf ; PIDS https://pidswebs.pids.gov.ph/CDN/PUBLICATIONS/pidsrp1501.pdf ; DSWD https://pantawid.dswd.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/4Ps_AnnualReport2019.pdf . Annual 2009-11, 2013, 2015-17 missing.
- ESC grantees: 975,229 (SY2017-18, 3,320 schools, PHP 8.30B); 1,044,399 (SY2018-19, 3,481 schools, PHP 9.63B) (https://peac.org.ph/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/PEAC-Biennial-Report-SY-2017-2018-to-SY-2018-2019.pdf). SHS voucher plus ESC private-school grantees 1.29M in SY2017-18 (PIDS DP 2019-13 https://pidswebs.pids.gov.ph/CDN/PUBLICATIONS/pidsdps1913.pdf), voucher PHP 17,500-22,500. Earlier years and GASTPE totals not found.
- SUCs (main campuses): 48 (1980); 107 (1999-00); 111 (2001-06); 110 (2006-09); 109 (2009-10); 112 (2013-14). Satellite campuses 247 (2001-02) -> 451 (2013-14). Public HEIs 298 (1980), 174 (1990-91), 405 (2001-02), 673 (2013-14), 263 (Jan 2024). Sources: https://pidswebs.pids.gov.ph/CDN/PUBLICATIONS/pidsdps1203.pdf ; https://pre.econ.upd.edu.ph/index.php/pre/article/download/958/847 ; https://pidswebs.pids.gov.ph/CDN/document/pidsdps2441.pdf . Note the 1980 and 1990-91 columns are not clearly comparable.
- School building: only the 2011-12 shortage (152,569 classrooms, PHP 104.5B), the 2017 Basic Education Facilities Fund transfer (>PHP 100B to DPWH) and QBEDP PPP counts found; no programme series.

## B6. DepEd flows
- SHS modelling: 54 schools; SY2013-14 5,756 Grade 11 and 4,246 Grade 12; SY2014-15 over 10,000 students (https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/?p=628212). Early implementers are therefore negligible in scale (about 10k vs 1.4M).
- Grade 11 SY2016-17: early count 1,300,938 (28 Jun 2016, https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/793017/1-3m-students-join-senior-high-fears-unfounded-deped-claims); 1,445,107 (783,452 public, 661,655 private) (https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/basic-education-reforms-and-the-k-to-12-program-in-the-philippines/90843382 slide 14); 1,193,002 as of 2 Aug 2016 (slide 33, unclear scope). About 1.5M Grade 10 completers in SY2015-16; DepEd expected about 400,000 not to proceed (https://philstar.com/headlines/2018/05/11/1814173/senior-high-school-results-exceeded-expectations-deped).
- Grade 10 completers 1,587,416; Grade 11 enrolees 1,481,103; transition 93.3% (https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/news/nation/652960/93-of-grade-10-students-transitioned-to-grade-11-says-deped/story/?amp). The article labels the completers SY2016-17 and Grade 11 SY2017-18, consistent with a cohort transition. SY2017-18 SHS: Grade 11 1.5M (52% DepEd, 45% private, 3% SUC/LUC), Grade 12 1.2M; first SHS graduates over 1.2M in 2018 (PIDS SHS seminar slides 13, 14, 31, https://pidswebs.pids.gov.ph/CDN/EVENTS/20200212_shs_process_evaluation_public_seminar.pdf).
- Pre-K-12 reference: secondary completion rate 75.24% (2008-09) to 74.81% (2012-13); secondary enrolment 6.76M to 7.11M (https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/Basic_Education_Statistics_in_the_Philippines.html). About 50% of HS completers went to college pre-K-12 (Inquirer link above). HS graduate counts by year: not found.

## B7. Delayed or returning entry
No age-of-entrant data found. Qualitative: CHED reported "a significant increase in freshmen who had not enrolled previously but later chose to attend" (2017) and acceleration of some students before SHS. PIDS DP 2025-12 (https://ideas.repec.org/p/phd/dpaper/dp_2025-12.html) studies exits of ever-enrolled college-age students; abstract has no entry-age figures. Household surveys (APIS/FLEMMS microdata) would be the route for age-at-entry.
