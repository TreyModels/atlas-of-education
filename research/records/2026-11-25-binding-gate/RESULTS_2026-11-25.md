# Binding-gate experiment — results (SIMULATION ONLY)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_BINDING_GATE.md` (commit `37cbe3d`, before any run). 1,800 runs:
quota seat share S ∈ {0.25,0.35,0.45,0.55,0.70} x 10 vacancy levels x 6 fee levels x 5 seeds, plus a
high-bar arm (score thresholds 14–17). 5 seeds/cell; seed CIs approximate.

| arm | mean Q5−Q1 tutoring gap | E8 fee effect on gap (fee .19 vs .00) |
|---|---|---|
| share_0.25 | 0.4419 | −0.0544 |
| share_0.35 | 0.4349 | −0.0603 |
| share_0.45 | 0.4238 | −0.0615 |
| share_0.55 | 0.3969 | −0.0693 |
| share_0.70 | 0.3426 | −0.0583 |
| bar_high (14–17) | 0.2308 | −0.2076 |

- **H1 SUPPORTED**: tutoring gap rises monotonically as seats shrink. Spearman ρ = **−1.00**;
  gap(S=0.25) − gap(S=0.70) = **+0.0993**.
- **H2 (primary, fee × scarcity interaction) UNDERPOWERED**: interaction = **+0.0039**,
  approx. SE 0.0119, 95% CI **[−0.0194, +0.0273]** — no detectable differential. The fee effect itself is
  negative at every seat share (fee abolition raises the wealth gap in the simulation), but the
  differential across scarcity is not resolvable with 5 seeds.
- The high-bar arm compresses the gap to 0.231 with a much larger negative fee effect — the **form** of
  the binding gate matters, not only its tightness.

SIMULATION ONLY; no causal language; the H2 null is reported as a null.
