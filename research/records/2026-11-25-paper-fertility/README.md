## Honesty and deviations

placebo keeps the framing descriptive in abstract, introduction, and conclusion. Supporting sections keep their
own nulls (cohort/pipeline attenuation contradicted; care loop null; strong no-off-switch falsified; no tipping

re-verified after the compute node went offline and is not used for any number here; the published battery covers
