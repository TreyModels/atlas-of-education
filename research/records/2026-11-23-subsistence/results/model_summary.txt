=== T3 joint demography-agriculture-absorber demonstration model ===
3 free parameters calibrated jointly on 1950-2020 stylised-country paths (cross-country medians);
Boppart inputs: sigma = 1.5 (estimated in T1); observed agricultural labour productivity is split
into TFP and input intensity; food demand has a subsistence floor and a low income elasticity.
N0 and kappa are calibrated directly from the TFR/survival/schooling endpoints (not free). A
demonstration model, not an estimate of any country's history.
