=== C5: US historical laboratory ===
Data availability: OWID long-run fertility (1800+) and mean years of schooling (1870+) are usable.
A long-run US agricultural-employment-share series was NOT obtainable (OWID share-of-employment series
starts 1991; four other OWID slugs and FRED returned 404/timeout). We therefore use the documented
Historic Statistics/USDA benchmark dates for the farm-employment share and label them external reference
points, not API data. The US order is read from the dated series below.
US fertility: 91 years 1933-2023; US schooling: 31 years 1870-2020
US farm-employment share benchmarks used (external reference): 1910:31.0%, 1920:27.0%, 1930:21.5%, 1940:17.0%, 1950:12.0%, 1960:8.0%, 1970:4.0%
US onset years: fertility decline 1957 (cohort 1937); schooling take-off 1955 (cohort 1935); farm-employment decline 1910 (cohort 1890).
US order: farm employment (1910) -> schooling (1955) -> fertility (1957)
Reading: in the observable US window the order is farm-employment decline (external benchmarks)
-> schooling -> fertility, which is consistent with the theory's ordering. But the OWID US fertility
series starts only in 1933, so this says nothing about the nineteenth-century US fertility transition,
and the farm-share benchmarks are external reference points, not API data. The US therefore supports
the ordering only for the twentieth century, and cannot adjudicate the earlier transition.
Cross-country (C4) is the opposite: in the common 1965-2010 window schooling take-off precedes
agricultural labour release in 61 of 64 countries, so the generational ordering is NOT supported
there - reported as the pre-registered falsifier result.

Second early industrialiser (UK): a long-run agricultural-employment series was not obtainable from the
same public sources; the gap is documented rather than filled with a proxy. OWID fertility/schooling for
the UK exist but without the farm-employment series the ordering cannot be dated, so no UK ordering is
claimed.
