# Synthetic panel: five expert perspectives on the six candidates

Each panelist scores each candidate 1–5 on "would a referee in my field accept this as new enough to carry a
paper?", names the strongest objection, and gives a verdict. The panelists are synthetic roles, not real people;
their function is to force the objections a real referee would raise. Scores are opinion, the inventory numbers
are evidence.

## P1 — Demographer (transition, fertility, DHS)

| candidate | score | strongest objection |
|---|---|---|
| 1 multiverse resolution rule | 2 | Not a demographic contribution; it is statistical method and belongs in a methods journal. |
| 2 schools before factories | 4 | The sequence is interesting and the case work real, but "schooling-first" is a dating convention: ties and coarse take-off dates decide it. Porzio et al. (2021) is close on the human side of structural change. |
| 3 GPU transition counterfactual | 4 | Transition demographers will accept the *demonstration* only if the paper is explicit that the result is a property of the calibrated model; the unresolved tension with the country-level child-survival link must be a section, not a footnote. |
| 4 Paper F levels-vs-changes audit | 4 | Referees at PDR will accept the audit if framed as an audit; Liu & Raftery (2020) is the closest competitor and the paper must say what it adds over their country-level model. |
| 5 rebound ceiling | 3 | The rebound is known (Goldstein et al. 2009; Myrskylä et al. 2009); the 396-episode test is an extension, not a new fact, and the ceiling is fragile. |
| 6 child-mortality volatility | 3 | The level relation is 1976-vintage; the volatility null is a nice negative but not a paper. |

**Panel's strongest single warning** (P2, endorsed by P5): the sequencing paper's evidence is joint determination —
schooling and agricultural release are both driven by industrialisation — so the paper must present the sequence
as a dating-and-assembly result with the supply/demand decomposition, never as a causal claim about schooling
causing the release. **Second warning** (P1, endorsed by P4): the transition counterfactual is model-structural;
the paper's value is the negative result (individuation does not beat development-only) plus the identification
limit, not the 3.2%.
