## Venue conventions consulted
*Journal of Comments and Replications in Economics* (JCRE) and *International Journal for Re-Research* (IJR)
publish short comments, replications and re-analyses; the conventions drawn on here — pre-registration,
reporting of deviations, a data-availability statement, and separate "replication / correction / new" labels —
are standard in that literature (see Christensen & Miguel, 2018; Mueller-Langer et al., 2019). Journal-level
submission formatting was not consulted directly in this session; the manuscript follows APA 7 and the section
order requested by the project.
