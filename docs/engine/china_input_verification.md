**Open question in the file resolved.** `_URB_M[2020][0] = 5.3` is **correct**. NBS spokesperson Mao Shengyong, reported by 央广网 on 16 March 2020: “2月份，调查失业率达到了6.2%（1月份为5.3%）” — and *China Economic Weekly* (中国经济周刊, 30 March 2020) puts February at “环比上升0.9个百分点” (6.2 − 0.9 = 5.3). The “up 1 point” in the English-language report is a rounded rendering of +0.9. The `UNRESOLVED` comment has been replaced with this resolution in the working tree (see the `_URB_CHECKED` note in `data_china.py`).

**One adjacent error found in an already-`v` value (not part of the `m` set).** `_URB_V[2023][0]` **was 5.6** in the 5 Nov version; the NBS value is **5.5** (now applied). NBS commentary by Wang Pingping (王萍萍), 19 April 2023: “1月份全国城镇调查失业率为5.5%”. The file's 2023-02 = 5.6 and 2023-03 = 5.3 are correct in the same sentence. See §2f and the JSON block.

**Definition issues settled.**

- **GRADUATES** is the *announced graduating-class scale used for employment planning* (“全国普通高校毕业生人数”), as the file already says — not realised regular-HEI graduates from the education communiqués. All 11 values match that series.
- **TERTIARY_ENTRANTS 2021 = 9.56 is correct** and the audit that produced it was right, for a reason worth recording: the 2021 communiqué reports 高职（专科）招生 552.58万人 **含** five-year-vocational transfers 45.20万人, while 2019/2020 say “另有…46.00/46.28万人” and 2022–2024 say **不含** 54.29/60.70/60.22万人. So 2021 is the one year whose published total embeds the transfers; stripping them gives 444.60 + 4.14 + (552.58 − 45.20) = **956.12万 = 9.56m**, comparable with the rest of the series. (The alternative — using the published 2021 total 1001.32 — would mix conventions.)
- **BIRTHS** are **not published annual counts**. They are reconstructed from two NBS tables (birth rate × mean of adjacent year-end populations) and should carry `d` (derived), not `v`, with the formula recorded in `SOURCES`. All 12 rounded values match; 2006 is 15.85, which the file rounds to 15.8 (see caveats).

## 4. How verified: what worked, what did not

**Worked.**

- **stats.gov.cn yearbook tables** — the single most useful site. `https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/ndsj/2011/html/D0301e.htm` (population, 10,000 persons) and `.../D0302e.htm` (birth/death/natural growth rates) both fetched (HTTP 200) and are machine-readable. This is what turned the births from memory into a reconstruction.
- **moe.gov.cn** — the annual communiqué index `https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/sjzl_fztjgb/` lists every 全国教育事业发展统计公报 from 2006 to 2025, and each article page fetches (HTTP 200) when requested over **https with redirects followed** (`curl -L`); plain `http://` requests to the old-style `jyb_xwfb` news URLs return 404. All 12 tertiary-entrant figures and the exact “含/不含五年制高职转入” wording came from these primary pages.
- **chsi.com.cn (学信网)** — hosts the MoE 2018 graduating-class announcement verbatim: “2018届全国普通高校毕业生预计820万人”.
- **Sina news / Xinhua reprints** — 2014-12-05 (749万 for 2015; 727万 for 2014) and 2016-03-10 (765万 for 2016, MoHRSS).
- **cnr.cn (央广网)** — the 2018-04-17 piece on the first release of the surveyed unemployment series (“1至3月分别为5.0%、5.0%和5.1%”) and the 2020-03-16 piece quoting NBS’s Mao Shengyong (“6.2%（1月份为5.3%）”). These are contemporaneous reports of NBS releases and they anchored both ends of the urban series.
- **gotohui.com (聚汇数据)** — a data aggregator that republishes the NBS monthly series **unrounded** (`201801`–`202605`, “数据来源：国家统计局”) and the annual 普通本专科招生数 (`1949`–`2015`). Useful for confirming the published one-decimal values and for spotting that the remembered values are the published ones (4.96→5.0, 5.06→5.1).
- **en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unemployment_in_China** — has the NBS monthly table (2018–2023, all ages, plus the 16–24 and 25–59 columns), whose table source is cited as “National Data, data.stats.gov.cn”. Its 1-decimal values agree with gotohui’s unrounded values on **53/53** overlapping months.
- **globaltimes.cn** — for the 2025 youth spot check (August 18.9, September 17.7, October 17.3).
- **stats.gov.cn commentary page** — NBS’s own 2023-04-19 Q1 commentary (`/sj/sjjd/202304/t20230419_1938809.html`) gave the 2023-01 value that caught the adjacent error.

**Did not work.**

- **data.stats.gov.cn (NBS National Data warehouse)** — HTTP 403 with `events … reason:UrlACL` from both the platform fetch proxy and a direct `curl` with browser headers, for the HTML query page and the JSON API alike. This is why the urban series is confirmed by transcription-plus-anchors rather than by one NBS page. `data_china.py` should still name NBS National Data as the origin (with the 2023-era URL), and record in `SOURCES` that the fetched evidence was the two transcriptions below.
- **ycharts.com** — HTTP 202 with an empty body (bot wall).
- **Several government/news URLs from `docs/input_audit_supplied.md`** — the moe.gov.cn `jyb_xwfb` links returned 404 (paths have moved); `gov.cn` app links 301 to an app page; `stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/` monthly release pages are JS-driven listings, and locating 48 individual monthly pages was not economical. The figures in the audit still checked out against other fetches, but its URLs should not be pasted into `SOURCES` without re-checking.
- **NBS monthly release pages for individual months** — not fetched. Where a monthly release was needed as an anchor, a contemporaneous 央广网 report of that release was used instead.

## 5. Caveats and open questions for the Lead

1. **Urban series citation strategy.** The values are right (53/53 agreement between two independent NBS transcriptions; seven pre-verified months unchanged; three anchor months from NBS reports). But the primary NBS database is unreachable from this network, so `SOURCES` should say the series was verified *via transcribed NBS National Data*, not that the NBS pages were read. If the MacBook session can reach `data.stats.gov.cn`, a single query (`cn=A01&zb=A0E01`) would upgrade the citation to primary in about a minute.
2. **2020-01** — the file’s open question is resolved in favour of **5.3**; the “up 1 point” wording in the English report is +0.9 rounded. Replace the `UNRESOLVED` comment.
3. **BIRTHS provenance** — recommend flag `d` (derived), formula recorded, values unchanged. 2006 is 15.8502; the file holds 15.8. Half-up rounding would give 15.9; the difference is 0.05m and not worth changing. The reconstruction uses the 2011 yearbook, i.e. pre-2020-census populations; a later yearbook would move individual values by a few hundredths of a million.
4. **TERTIARY_ENTRANTS convention** — 2021 = 9.56 is correct given the series excludes five-year-vocational transfers (the 2022–24 communiqués say 不含 explicitly; 2019–20 say 另有; 2021 alone says 含 and must be adjusted). The pre-2019 totals do not state their treatment, so record the possible small level break in `SOURCES` rather than rebuilding.
5. **GRADUATES 2008–2013 and 2017** rest on a single secondary compilation (China Education Online) that is consistent with the other anchors and with the widely reported official series; 2014–2016 and 2018 have independent Xinhua/MoE anchors. If the Lead wants every year on a primary page, the MoE/人社部 annual “就业创业工作网络视频会议” releases for those years would do it, but that is more search than this pass could justify.
6. **Adjacent correction** — 2023-01, 5.6 → **5.5** (NBS 王萍萍 commentary). Outside the `m` set but should be applied in the same batch.
