## What this establishes

1. **The level is now right.** With the cutoff after the definition change, the test bias is -1.47 pts over 2025-26. Every Dec 2022 test missed by 4-6 pts in one direction.
2. **It still loses to naive:** 1.71 against 0.89 for the same month of 2024. The whole gap is seasonal amplitude. The model drains unemployment through spring too fast (about 2 pts too low, Mar-May) and adds the graduating class too abruptly in July (about 3 pts too high). The same pattern shows in the 2024 fit, so this is structural and not an error in the input forecasts.
3. **The fit is strained:** about 6 of 11 parameters sit at bounds, and wage speed lam_g swings from 0.004 to 1 between weightings. The seasonal shape cannot be fitted within this structure. Candidates: (a) a stock of longer-term unemployed graduates whose job-finding does not rise in spring (duration dependence); (b) graduates entering the measured labour force over several months after July rather than at once (exam and postgraduate-application calendars); (c) new-definition counting of students who graduate mid-year. Each gives a different spring and July signature.
