## What this establishes

1. **Selected on training fit alone** (new-method 2024 MAE, ties broken by old-method fit): **spread8**. Test MAE 0.99 against naive 0.89: the first China specification in this project to beat the naive benchmark out of sample, on a like-for-like definition.
2. **The mechanism is spread-out entry.** Every variant with graduates entering over 8-11 months beats naive (0.62-0.70); the base model (1.36) and duration dependence alone (1.04) do not. Spreading the entry removes most of the spring and July errors. Read substantively, a large share of each graduating class enters the measured labour market over the following academic year rather than in July. That fits the postgraduate and civil-service exam calendars and the waiting behaviour the Tilly model was built for.
3. **Not yet decisive.** 19 test months; the paired t is +0.66. A July error of +1.7 pts remains. Each new NBS release extends the test, and the claim should be rescored then, without refitting.
4. **This is not a blind test.** The five variants were fixed before these fits ran, and the winner is chosen by training fit. But the candidate mechanisms came from forecast_2024.md, which had already shown the test-period seasonal errors next to the fit-window ones. The same pattern is visible within the 2024 fit window alone, so the diagnosis did not need the test months, but they had been seen. The first blind check is the next NBS releases (Oct 2026 onwards), scored with the parameters frozen in seasonal_2024.json.
