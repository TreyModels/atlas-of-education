## What this establishes

1. **The child labour gap is not a calibration-ridge problem.** Every variant gives about 14%, even when the out-of-school targets are roughly halved (census2019). The school-value constants do not set the level. The work side does: child earnings, the taste for work, and the 35% hazardous share applied to 15-17s. A retune aimed at 10.7% must free those, and the 2012 survey must be put on the same definition as the model.
2. **Schools as protection ranks first everywhere**, but its size comes from placeholder magnitudes (TODO: Bjørn). It is robust to the calibration ridge, not to those magnitudes.
3. **Cash and the factory-only ban lower child labour in every variant and seed.** Their size varies by a factor of 1.5-2.5.
4. **The Thai border closure and the garment boom have no established sign.** Both are within seed noise and flip across variants. The interface should not present a direction for them without the 30-seed bands.
5. **The targets disagree.** UNESCO UIS 2024 puts lower-secondary out-of-school at 17.6%. The 2019 census attendance gives about 8.4% for ages 12-14. Definitions differ: UIS counts by level and the census by age, among other differences. That observation equation must be settled before any retune.
