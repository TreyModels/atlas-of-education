Reading: S1 summing well below 1 means interactions carry the outcome; ST much larger than S1 marks a parameter that matters only in combination. A parameter outside every stiff list cannot be pinned down by matching these outcomes, so it stays a declared assumption.

**Caveat:** at n=64 some first-order S1 estimates fall outside [0, 1] (negative, or above 1); that is estimator noise, not a share. Read the total-order ST and its bootstrap interval for the stiff/sloppy ordering; do not read the S1 point values as variance shares at this n. A larger n (256+) is needed for precise shares.
